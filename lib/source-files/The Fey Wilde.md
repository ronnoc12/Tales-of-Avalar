\#\# 🎭 How to Tell a Spy Sapling from a Normal Sapling

\#\#\# \*\*1. Compliment Test\*\*

A regular sapling reacts like a plant.

A spy sapling reacts like it’s on a first date.

\* Compliment its leaves →

  \* Normal sapling: \*rustles slightly\*  
  \* Spy sapling: leaves blush a different color, leans closer, whispers

    \> \*“Oh—well—no one’s ever noticed before…”\*

If it says \*anything\* unprompted, it’s a spy.

\---

\#\#\# \*\*2. The Secret Test\*\*

Spy saplings are incapable of \*\*not\*\* listening.

Have a party member loudly announce a \*\*fake secret\*\*, such as:

\* “Don’t tell anyone, but I’m actually three goblins in a coat”  
\* “I think the Archfey smells like wet cinnamon”  
\* “The moon owes me money”

Then wait.

Within 1d4 minutes:

\* A squirrel repeats it  
\* A mushroom giggles  
\* The wind whispers it back wrong

Trace the gossip trail → spy sapling nearby.

\---

\#\#\# \*\*3. Ask It a Question Plants Shouldn’t Answer\*\*

This works disturbingly well.

Ask:

\* “Hey, what day is it?”  
\* “Do you know who’s watching us?”  
\* “What did you hear earlier?”

Normal sapling: nothing.

Spy sapling:

\* pauses too long  
\* leaves point \*slightly\* toward the thing it’s watching  
\* says something like

  \> \*“I couldn’t possibly say… but if someone WERE watching, they’d be over there.”\*

Plants should not hedge.

\---

\#\#\# \*\*4. The Rhythm Problem\*\*

Spy saplings are synced to Fey courts.

Have someone clap, hum, or tap a rhythm.

\* Normal saplings sway randomly  
\* Spy saplings fall into \*\*perfect, unnatural tempo\*\*

If the tree is keeping time better than the bard, it’s compromised.

\---

\#\#\# \*\*5. The Hat Test (Extremely Reliable)\*\*

Put a hat on the sapling.

\* Normal sapling: the hat falls  
\* Spy sapling: \*\*adjusts it\*\*

If the sapling \*tips the hat\* — that’s espionage.
