Midcross Council Members 

\#\#\# Council Member Breakdown

\#\#\#\# 1\. \*\*Councilor Doran Blackstone (Head of Infrastructure)\*\*  
\- \*\*Disposition\*\*: Pragmatic and meticulous. Doran is known for his focus on efficiency and reliability but can be seen as somewhat aloof.  
\- \*\*Description\*\*: Doran is responsible for overseeing the city’s infrastructure, including defensive walls, transportation routes, and public utilities. His role makes him crucial in maintaining the city's operational stability.  
\- \*\*Political Affiliations\*\*: Generally neutral but has been known to advocate for technological upgrades and modernization.  
\- \*\*Ties to the City\*\*: Doran is a long-standing member of the council with deep roots in the city's engineering community. His family has been involved in infrastructure work for generations.

\#\#\#\# 2\. \*\*Councilor Elira Stormwind (Chief Diplomat)\*\*  
\- \*\*Disposition\*\*: Charismatic and idealistic. Elira is passionate about diplomacy and believes in the power of alliances and external support.  
\- \*\*Description\*\*: As the Chief Diplomat, Elira is responsible for managing foreign relations and negotiating treaties. She is well-regarded for her diplomatic skills and her ability to forge important alliances.  
\- \*\*Political Affiliations\*\*: Elira is generally supportive of magic and pushes the city to consider reshaping some of its “archaic” perspectives to better serve its citizens.   
\- \*\*Ties to the City\*\*: Elira has a reputation for being a devoted public servant who genuinely wants to see Midcross thrive. 

\#\#\#\# 3\. \*\*Council Member Aleron Drake (Head of Defense)\*\*  
\- \*\*Disposition\*\*: Stoic and disciplined. Aleron is a seasoned warrior with a no-nonsense approach to security and defense.  
\- \*\*Description\*\*: Aleron oversees the city’s military and defense strategies, ensuring that the city is prepared for external threats. He is respected for his strategic mind and commitment to the city’s safety.  
\- \*\*Political Affiliations\*\*: Generally conservative, with a focus on traditional defense methods and skepticism towards external influences.  
\- \*\*Ties to the City\*\*: Aleron is a veteran of the city’s guard, with a deep personal connection to the safety of Midcross. His family has a long history of military service.

\#\#\#\# 4\. \*\*Councilor Marla Thorn (Head of Commerce)\*\*  
\- \*\*Disposition\*\*: Pragmatic and business-oriented. Marla is focused on the economic stability and growth of the city.  
\- \*\*Description\*\*: Marla manages trade, industry, and economic policies. Her role involves ensuring that the city’s economy remains robust and prosperous.  
\- \*\*Political Affiliations\*\*: Often aligned with policies that promote economic growth and trade, but less involved in security matters.  
\- \*\*Ties to the City\*\*: Marla has been influential in fostering economic ties with neighboring regions and is known for her work in revitalizing the city’s trade routes.

\#\#\#\# 5\. \*\*Councilor Cedric Vale (Head of Public Works)\*\*  
\- \*\*Disposition\*\*: Detail-oriented and community-focused. Cedric is dedicated to improving the city’s infrastructure and public services.  
\- \*\*Description\*\*: Cedric oversees public works projects, including maintenance of roads, buildings, and public amenities.  
\- \*\*Political Affiliations\*\*: Neutral, but supportive of projects that enhance the quality of life for the city’s residents.  
\- \*\*Ties to the City\*\*: Cedric is known for his hands-on approach to his role and his commitment to improving public services.
