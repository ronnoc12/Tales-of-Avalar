Triq and Vargeez 

After the defeat at midcross and the discovery of their home the goblins regrouped and fled to the south towards the lands of the wondering grove. Many died on the journey some stayed behind unwilling to travel. About 20%of their people died on the way. When they arrived the landscape was wilder than they were used to with no easy targets to attack they struggled to adapt to the new landscape coming from a mountainous region to the dense forest. In this new unfamiliar landscape they struggled further to build a new and even more of their people perished. 

Triq leading the charge got her people to their new shelter but decided she needed to gain power to end the suffering of her people so she journeyed away to find the power she desperately seeks abandoning her people. 

For a time Vargeez did his best to survive many many died leading him to a point of desperation the starved goblin tribe eventually came across members of the wondering grove attempting to hold them up but after the many months of hard travel instability and starvation they hardly posed any threat at all. Seeing the desperation in their eyes (head of wondering grove section) took pity on them. They shared food, recourses, taught them how to hunt and trap the animals of their land. 

At first it was Vargeezes intention to use the humans then once they learned all they can to betray them, each day he would tell himself tomorrow they'll take the humans down but each new morning the reality of the equality and kindness they were shown by the grove kept him from taking action. All Vargeezes ever wanted was equality and prosperity for his people how could he defiled that which his people struggled so hard for. 

Each conversation with (head of grove) lead him more and more to realize that acceptance had found them and asking his people to die to be accepted elsewhere was a fools errand and without Triq to fan the flames most of the clan migrated to this new way of thinking, some still harbor hatred in their hearts for the pink skins (eventually lead to a mutiny if unchecked) awaiting Triqs return or a new leader to take them to victory. 

Vargeezes and many of the goblins now travel with the grove, embracing their way of life living in harmony with the land. 

Triq: 

Triq decides to seek out the ultimate help she seeks out a long forgotten temple to Orcus seeking his grimoir to make a deal that can save her people. Orcus gives her the power to resurrect her dead empowering them to fight for those who remain. All of Triqs constructs now use hellfire steal to shuttle the souls of those killed to her patron. It takes her a great deal of time to find this place and gain this power but once she does she will return to raise her people from the dead to fight for her once more.

### **🔥 Orcus – Demon Lord of Undeath**

* **Domain**: Undeath, necromancy, hatred for life itself.

* **Why Orcus?**

  * Orcus is ideal if Triq wants **raw destructive power**, especially the power to **raise her fallen people**.

  * The deaths on the road? She could have **refused to let them go**, and with Orcus’ power, reanimated them into an undead goblin horde.

  * Orcus **demands souls**, and Triq would become his chief harbinger, culling life to feed his domain.

* **The Pact**:

  * Orcus might grant her the ability to **create a new goblin empire built from the bones of the old**.

  * This could turn into a horrifying moral challenge for the players—**liberated goblins who are actually undead**.

  * Orcus loves despair—Triq’s tragic fall would be a perfect feather in his cap.
