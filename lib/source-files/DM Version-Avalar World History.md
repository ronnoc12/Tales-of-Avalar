***\*\*Avalar World History:\*\****

\*\*Ancient Era:\*\*

\- \*\*The Age of Primordial Chaos:\*\* In the distant past, the world was a realm of untamed magic and primal forces. Mythical creatures roamed freely, and the veil between planes was thin, allowing for frequent interplanar crossings. This era is shrouded in mystery and legend, with tales of powerful beings and cataclysmic events shaping early civilizations.

During this era great primordial beings walked the planes freely crossing between the boundaries This primordial era was a time when Avalar was a world in flux, constantly shaped by the raw, untamed forces of magic and nature. The Sylvani, vast tree-like entities, and the Charr, beings made of flame, were not simply peaceful races—they were the living embodiments of Avalar’s nascent elements. The Sylvani's initial rise was prompted by a need to adapt to a rapidly changing environment; they had once been an aquatic species, thriving in the vast oceans of Avalar, but the planet’s changing atmosphere forced them to adapt to the land. Their arrival on the land created an unprecedented ecological shift, altering the balance of elemental forces. The Charr, born of fire, had no natural affinity with the Sylvani’s growing dominance over the air. The constant consumption of oxygen by the Sylvani weakened the once harmonious relationship between fire and flame, leading the Charr to attack the Sylvani in a brutal and devastating war that scorched the earth and split continents. It is said the "Pearl of Avalar," a magical artifact created during this war, was the key to both the Sylvani’s and the Charr’s eventual fall. The Pearl shattered, scattering its essence throughout Avalar, leaving behind dangerous relics, strange phenomena, and magical scars that would affect the future of the world for eons.

\#\#\# \*\*Era of the Shattered Veil: Dawn of the Modern Races\*\*  
\---

\#\#\# \*\*Overview:\*\*  
The \*\*Era of the Shattered Veil\*\* marks a pivotal turning point in the history of Avalar. As the veil between planes begins to thicken and the Pearl of Avalar shatters, the realms of the material world and the planes of existence are no longer as easily traversed. The once-fluid boundaries between the planes of existence are now harder to cross, limiting the flow of primordial energies and preventing the frequent interactions with powerful planar entities. This era witnesses the rise of the first modern races, as hunter-gatherer tribes and nomadic groups slowly evolve into the beginnings of organized civilizations. These newly emerging peoples tap into the elemental magics left behind by the primordial forces, using them to craft tools, weapons, and rudimentary spells that aid in their survival and growth.

\#\#\# \*\*Key Events of the Era:\*\*

1\. \*\*The Shattering of the Pearl of Avalar:\*\*  
   The event known as \*\*The Shattering\*\* is a cataclysmic moment in Avalar's history. The \*\*Pearl of Avalar\*\*, a vast and mysterious artifact of immense magical power, was once the focal point of magical and planar energy. It served as a conduit that kept the veil between the planes thin, allowing for the frequent movement of energies and beings across the realms.    
     
   However, during the final days of the \*\*Primordial Era\*\*, the Pearl of Avalar shattered under the weight of a cosmic struggle. The reasons for the shattering remain a mystery, but its consequences were immediate and profound. The veils between the planes thickened dramatically, making interplanar travel more difficult and dangerous. Magical creatures, demigods, and planar entities that once walked freely among mortals were now trapped within their realms, unable to interact as directly as they had before. The resulting rupture caused a ripple effect, altering the very fabric of Avalar's magic. 

2\. \*\*The Formation of the Obelisks:\*\*  
   As a result of the shattering of the Pearl, \*\*shards\*\* of the Pearl scattered across Avalar, embedded deeply into the landscape. These shards are not just pieces of the Pearl, but immense \*\*magical artifacts\*\*, each capable of altering the flow of magic in their vicinity. Over time, these shards would come to be known as \*\*Obelisks\*\*, towering structures of crystalline energy that pulse with the remnants of primordial magic. They became key locations where the veil between the planes is most accessible, and their presence marks places of significant magical power.

   The \*\*Obelisks\*\* would come to be revered, feared, and studied. These magical constructs could amplify or suppress magical abilities within their range, creating hotspots of intense magical potential. Some Obelisks could warp the very laws of nature, turning a once fertile land into a desolate desert, or vice versa. The Obelisks also became focal points for the new races, who would eventually learn to harness the powers of these ancient artifacts.

3\. \*\*Emergence of Modern Races:\*\*  
   As the primal chaos of the world began to settle into more defined patterns, the first \*\*modern races\*\* began to emerge from the remnants of older civilizations. These early races were a mixture of primal forces, influenced by the leftover elemental energies of the primordial world, and the newer, more structured forms of life that began to shape themselves from this chaos.

4\. \*\*Harnessing the Primordial Energies:\*\*  
   In this era, the remnants of the \*\*primordial elemental magics\*\* became a key aspect of the development of the new civilizations. As the veil thickened and the planes began to separate, the magical energies left behind by the primordial forces became more concentrated in certain regions, especially near the Obelisks. These energies were primal in nature—earth, fire, air, water, and aether—and began to influence the inhabitants of Avalar.

   \- \*\*Elemental Magic Use:\*\* The early humans, elves, and dwarves slowly began to harness these energies to create tools and perform basic magic. Early forms of \*\*elemental spellcasting\*\* were focused primarily on utility and survival—creating fire for warmth, manipulating the earth for shelter, and using water to cultivate crops. 

   \- \*\*First Civilizations:\*\* The scattered hunter-gatherer tribes slowly began to coalesce into early civilizations, with the first towns and cities beginning to form near Obelisks or places rich in primordial magical energy. These civilizations used the elements around them to establish trade routes, construct fortifications, and even defend against powerful creatures that once roamed freely before the shattering of the Pearl. 

5\. \*\*The Rise of the First Kingdoms:\*\*  
   With the use of magic, the mastery of elemental forces, and the discovery of the Obelisks, the foundations of the first kingdoms were laid. Early kingdoms formed around the Obelisks, where the veil between the planes was most accessible. These were places of power, where the flow of magic was strongest and where the first true rulers emerged—figures who could wield magic not just as a tool, but as a weapon. These early kings and queens often descended from the first tribes and grew in power through their control of these magical sites.

\*\*Classical Era:\*\*

\- \*\*Rise of Ancient Empires:\*\* As mortal civilizations began to flourish, great empires such as the Elven Dominion, Dwarven Kingdoms, and Human City-States emerged. Each race developed unique magical traditions, architectural marvels, and expansive realms. The era was marked by cultural exchange, trade, and occasional conflicts over resources and territory.

Rise of Ancient Empires: As the world recovered from the primordial chaos, civilizations arose from the remnants of earlier wars. The Elven Dominion, renowned for their mastery of nature magic and their ageless beauty, built vast cities hidden within the dense forests and other hard to reach places thesestunning hidden towering cities of crystal. The Dwarven Kingdoms, dug deep into the mountains, became known for their craftsmanship, mining, and the forging of powerful enchanted metals. Humans, ever adaptable, built sprawling city-states that traded and waged war with both elves and dwarves for control of resources.

The War of Aether Crystals: A conflict that spanned across centuries, this war erupted due to the discovery of rare "Aether Crystals," which held vast magical properties capable of manipulating the very fabric of reality. Both elves and dwarves fought fiercely for control over these valuable resources, and this conflict is often cited as one of the reasons for the eventual collapse of many of the ancient empires.

The Fall of the Elven Dominion: The collapse of the Elven Dominion was a slow and tragic event, exacerbated by internal strife, environmental changes, and overuse of magic. The once-mighty empire fractured as factions vied for control of the fading magical essence of their lands. Some scholars believe the Sundering was triggered by the misuse of the Aether Crystals by the elves, leading to the cataclysm that reshaped Avalar. Elves attempted to violate the nature of mortality using these crystals only to be struck down for their huberis. 

\- \*\*The Sundering Cataclysm:\*\* A catastrophic event, known as the Sundering, fractured the continents and reshaped the landscape. It was triggered by a magical experiment gone awry, unleashing destructive energies that devastated civilizations and altered the course of history. This event led to the decline of ancient empires and the rise of new powers in the aftermath.

Sundering Cataclysm:  
The Sundering: The Sundering marked a decisive moment in Avalar's history. In their search for power and eternal life, the elves sought to tap into forbidden magics using the Aether Crystals. The event fractured Avalar's continents, sending parts of the world spiraling into the void, while others were raised into the sky. Great tectonic shifts tore through the lands, leaving behind uninhabitable wastelands and creating new seas and mountains. Entire civilizations were wiped out, and magical ley lines that once provided power became chaotic and unpredictable. This event caused the world to enter a dark age, as all the major civilizations collapsed or withdrew into isolation. However, a few resilient empires managed to survive in the aftermath, such as the Kingdom of Keldor and the Daughters of Deshret, marking the beginning of the Medieval Era.

\*\*Medieval Era:\*\*

\- \*\*The Era of Darkness:\*\* Following the Sundering, the world plunged into an age of darkness and chaos. Magical energies surged unpredictably, spawning wild monsters and phenomena. Mages were feared and persecuted, blamed for the cataclysmic events. Cities erected barriers and wards to protect themselves from magical incursions, leading to the isolation and marginalization of magic users. 

\- \*\*The Founding of Deshret and the Udjat Camps:\*\* In the city of Deshret, situated in the desert, mages were systematically persecuted. The Udjat Camps were established to imprison and exploit mages, stripping them of their tongues and mutilating their hands to prevent spellcasting. This brutal practice continued for centuries, leaving a legacy of fear and resentment among magic users.

As fear of all magic worked itself into a frenzy humanoids everywhere vegan to expand and indulge their paranoia beginning to fear creatures with innate magical abilities. This lead to the systematic and aggressive genocide of dragons from the earth. Always chromatic dragons and humans had been at odd with one another for resources and territory but armies from multiple neighboring city centers would band together to hunt and exterminate this perceived magical threat. 

They believed dragons to be innate magical beings that not only possesses magic but some conjured or otherwise collected it to use their abilities. Some even went so far as to believe that by exterminating the dragons they could cure humanity's magical afflictions entirely. They were wrong of course and all the dragons have long since fled or been slain. Forced into plains unfamiliar to them and the farthest reaches of uncivilized society they are now believed to be extinct although this is not actually true. Many found ways of hiding or extending their lifespans far beyond their years.

Many fled to the Fae wild where some managed to seek refuge and integrate with the Fae. Others however were too prideful angry they'd been displaced by vermin such as humanoids and took their frustrations out on the Fae. Attempting to force the Fae to bend to their will and give up their territories to the dragons. But Fae are not one to be trifled with. They fought back hard, and the chromatic dragons which couldn't be reasoned with were dealt with although a great deal of sacrifice was made to achieve this. Many lost their lives and thier homes but a few brave Fae banished or killed the invading force preserving the independence of the Fae Wilds for now. 

\*\*Reformation Era:\*\*

\- \*\*Rise of The Unsighted Order:\*\* Amidst persecution, a nomadic group of marked mages, led by a visionary leader, founded The Unsighted Order. This leader, whose name is now lost to history, dedicated his life to finding a path for mages to harness and control their chaotic energies for positive purposes. The Unsighted Orders mission was to find a path for mages to control and harness their chaotic energies. They discovered ancient obelisks that could tame the wild magic, laying the foundation for a safer environment for magic users and initiating the Reformation period.

\- \*\*Establishment of The Celestial Alliance:\*\* Witnessing the rise of magical instability and extra-planar threats, The Celestial Alliance was founded under the guidance of a powerful celestial deity. Their mission was to investigate the root causes of these disturbances and provide humanitarian aid. The Alliance quickly gained political influence and became a beacon of hope during times of crisis.

\*\*Modern Era:\*\*

\- \*\*Rise of the Arcane Concordance:\*\*  
Centuries later, the ameliorate had not only reintegrated into society but had also advanced technology through their mastery of magic. This era saw the transformation of The Unsighted Order into the Arcane Concordance—a prestigious university and research center led by Faythe Kazula, a visionary leader who believed in elevating magic users to positions of influence and respect. The Arcane Concordance became a beacon of knowledge and innovation, pushing the boundaries of magical and scientific understanding.
