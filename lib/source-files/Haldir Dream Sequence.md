Haldir Dream Sequence 

\#\#\# \*\*Recurring Dream Arc: “Ash Beneath Bloom”\*\*

\---

\#\#\# \*\*DREAM 1: “The Smoke Before the Flame” (Glave’s Weakest Point)\*\*

\*Haldir’s first vision after consuming a soul—mostly symbolic, brief, emotionally charged.\*

\*\*Setting\*\*: A field of ash. A single \*\*tree with silver leaves\*\* stands untouched at the center, bleeding sap that burns like acid. \*\*Nyxis’s laugh\*\* echoes faintly but is cut short with a sharp gasp. The world rumbles. Something massive is overhead, but it never appears—only a \*\*shadow moving across the sky\*\*.

\*\*Sensory details\*\*:

\* Haldir tastes \*\*iron and roses\*\* on the air.  
\* A child’s whisper: \*“You promised.”\*  
\* A glint of steel—his glave—\*\*embedded in the trunk of the bleeding tree\*\*.

A voice flickers in his head, broken and staticky like a poor signal:

\> "…burns in silence… not dragon… \*not true… false scale… lies in bloom…\*"

Haldir awakens with his hand clenched around the glave’s hilt—even if he wasn’t holding it when he fell asleep.

\---

\#\#\# \*\*DREAM 2: “The Garden of Split Tongues”\*\*

\*Occurs after a second dragon soul is consumed. The glave is stronger—its intent is clearer.\*

\*\*Setting\*\*: A \*\*twisted Feywild garden\*\*—vivid, overgrown, but with creeping patches of \*\*rot and flame\*\*. He sees \*\*Nyxis\*\* dancing with fey children beneath glowing blossoms. But every time he tries to call her name, his voice comes out like a \*\*dragon’s roar\*\*.

He sees a \*\*dragon-shaped reflection in a fey pond\*\*, made of vines and fog. Its face shifts through different draconic forms—metallic, chromatic, gem—but its eyes are always \*\*his own\*\*.

The children laugh, then scream.

Nyxis turns to him and whispers, her voice layered with another beneath it:

\> “Something is wearing their skin.”

The glave’s voice creeps through like a whisper in his spine:

\> “The false Wyrm… dances beneath the roots… its fire reaches… to her door… \*cut the root before the bloom rots.\*”

Then: a fleeting image—\*\*a broken fang, a crown of thorns, a cave made of glass.\*\*

Haldir wakes up with the scent of smoke on his clothes.

\---

\#\#\# \*\*DREAM 3: “The Bloom That Burns”\*\*

\*The glave is nearing the strength to speak plainly. After 3–4 dragon souls. This is a full-blown vision quest.\*

\*\*Setting\*\*: The \*\*same glade\*\* where Nyxis lives—but it's frozen in time. A dragon’s body, stitched together with scales of every color, is \*\*coiled around the grove\*\*, sleeping. Its breath turns flowers to cinders. Haldir walks through the glade, and each step echoes like a war drum.

He passes through \*\*hallucinations\*\*:

\* Nyxis, bleeding from the mouth, saying: \*“You let it grow.”\*  
\* The little girl, her throat closed with vines, eyes wide.  
\* A \*\*mirror\*\* showing Haldir’s reflection… wearing black, dragon-forged armor, the glave pulsing with light.

Then, the glave speaks in full for the first time:

\> “False blood corrupts true flame. The impostor must fall… before the fire finds her heart.”

\> “Its soul is ripe. You must take it. Bind it to us. End its lie. Protect the bloom.”

Then quieter, to itself:

\> “…soon… your vessel will be ready.”

\*\*Final image\*\*: The bleeding tree from the first dream—only now it's Nyxis, crucified upon its branches, her blood growing flowers that burn away as they bloom.

Haldir wakes screaming, the glave hot in his hand.

\#\#\# \*\*Alternate Dream 2A: “The Mirror of Ash”\*\*

\*(Variant of Dream 2 – includes his sister in the garden)\*

\*\*Setting\*\*: The warped Feywild garden again. This time, it’s \*\*snowing ash\*\*, and the sky bleeds streaks of green and violet. Haldir walks through statues of his \*\*friends and family\*\*, all carved in white stone. Most are featureless—except two.

\*\*Nyxis\*\*, encased in crystal, her hand reaching out.

\*\*His sister\*\*, kneeling in a circle of mirrors. Each reflection shows her at different ages—laughing, training, crying—but one shows her face \*\*burned and scaled\*\*, turning to ash.

She looks at him and says in a voice like cracked porcelain:

\> “Your blade cuts paths you can’t see.”

Then the mirrors begin to \*\*scream\*\*—his own voice coming from each one, yelling commands, battle cries, regrets.

The glave murmurs, weak but growing:

\> “False scales… false hearts… trust no gleam that blinds…”

Then: a flash of the \*\*broken fang\*\*, but now it drips blood—\*his sister’s blood\*.

\---

\#\#\# \*\*Alternate Dream 3A: “The House on Fire”\*\*

\*(Variant of Dream 3 – more grounded and emotional, strong sister presence)\*

\*\*Setting\*\*: A dream-version of \*\*Haldir’s childhood home\*\*, but everything is \*\*on fire\*\*. He walks through the hallways, which are both familiar and strange. Paintings twist into dragon eyes. Furniture melts into bone.

He finds \*\*his sister\*\* in the training yard, standing still, her blade drawn but pointed at her own heart. Her armor flickers between its real form and \*\*dragon-scale\*\*.

She looks at him—not angry, but afraid:

\> “Why does it always fall to you to decide who burns?”

The fire rises, and from behind her, a shadowy \*\*draconic form\*\* emerges—too large, shifting. Not quite real. The glave hums louder than ever.

Then the voice speaks:

\> “You’ve not yet drawn the true fire. One still wears the bloom.”

\> “One still lies in light.”

The dragon-form lunges for his sister—he screams, tries to move—\*\*wakes up in a cold sweat\*\*, hand clutching the glave, which now has a faint crimson rune glowing near the pommel.

\#\#\# \*\*Post-Kill Dream: “Fractured Roots”\*\*

\*(After the first “false dragon” is slain – clarity twists into new uncertainty)\*

You see a beautiful field filled with flower blooms and the slight iridescent haze of the fey wild, you hear a giggle in the distance, Nyxis’s, you realize it's her grove, your home away from home, you feel comfort for a moment, but that moment is fleeting. Something’s \*different\*. The scene is peaceful, blooming. You see Nyxie standing before a large tree, healthy, smiling. For a moment, all is well.

Then Nyxie's laugh is cut short, she vanishes, her eyes going wide as she disappears, leaving only a scorched hollow in the ground where she once stood. 

Echoing around you you hear: “One root severed. But the rot runs deeper.”

A trail of \*\*flowers begins to bloom from the scotched hollow\*\*, stretching across a burned field into a mountain pass where the clouds form the skull of an unbelievably large beast with long winding horns on either side of its long narrow skull.

You follow the trail—and at its end is a figure Dana armored and, alone, surrounded by amorphous molten glass statues, She is weeping.

She turns and speaks—but her \*\*voice is not her own\*\*

 “The fire seeks its kin”

Then, a final image:

\* A battlefield.  
\* Haldir, standing alone.  
\* Nyxis, his sister, and the glave—each standing on opposite sides.  
\* And in the sky above them: \*\*the shadow of an enormous creature with large outstretched  wings\*\*, wings of fractured memories, descending closer to the cloud cover. 

He wakes with a single word \*\*burned into his mind\*\*:

\> \*"Decide."\*

\#\#\# \*\*Dream Sequence: "Ash Beneath Bloom"\*\*

The dream begins in \*\*Nyxis’s glade\*\*, a serene, moonlit grove in the Feywilds. The air shimmers with lilac mist, and luminous petals drift through the air. Haldir hears the faint laughter of Nyxis in the distance—soft, melodic, and distant. He steps forward through silvergrass, drawn to her voice.

But with each step, the dream begins to distort. The color drains from the trees, the petals curl and burn midair, and the laughter twists into a scream. He turns a corner and sees the grove \*\*engulfed in flames\*\*, emerald fire crackling unnaturally. In the center of it all is a massive \*\*shadowed dragon\*\*, wreathed in dying feylight, looming over Nyxis, who lies unconscious in a circle of scorched blossoms.

Suddenly, \*\*the glave speaks\*\*, not aloud, but within his mind like a whispered command woven with silk and venom:

\> “This one burns with prophecy. A \*False Wyrm\*, born of betrayal and rot. Its flame touches more than bark and branch… it seeks your heart, through hers.”

Images flash like blades:

\* Nyxis coughing blood in Haldir’s arms.  
\* His sister’s face turning away from him in disappointment.  
\* The little girl from his past—her neck slit, her eyes wide in betrayal, whispering:

\> “You said you’d protect me.”

He turns and sees \*\*the same little girl\*\*, now in fey garb, cradling a charred squirrel in her lap.

\> “You’re always too late,” she says.

The glave now takes on the \*\*visage of a knight in obsidian armor\*\*, standing beside him.

\> “You wield me, yet hesitate. You seek peace, but forget—peace is built upon the bones of monsters. This dragon’s soul is corruption. It festers in your world, and through it, all you love will be consumed. Strike first. Spare none.”

The knight raises the glave—and it’s Haldir’s own hands holding it now. Blood drips from the blade.

Suddenly, he's on a battlefield. A massive, sickly dragon rears before him, more spectral than flesh, with mismatched wings and a shifting form—\*\*a chimera of all dragons it has devoured\*\*. He feels a \*pull\*, the glave vibrating in his grasp like it’s alive, \*hungry\*.

\> “This one is near. A breath away in your waking world. Hunt it. End it. Or watch everything burn.”

Just before he wakes, the dream ends with Nyxis reaching out to him from within a collapsing grove, her eyes clouded with draconic slit pupils as she whispers:

\> “You didn’t stop it, my love…”
