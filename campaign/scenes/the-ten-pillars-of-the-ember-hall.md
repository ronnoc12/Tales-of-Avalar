{prep}Where is the Ember Hall? (Magma-lit, so possibly within the [[Ashvault Sanctum]].){/prep}

## Read-Aloud
Each pillar is ten feet tall, faced with cracked tiles of gemstone and volcanic glass, faintly reflective in the magma's glow. The earliest are bleached and worn smooth; the newer ones keep sharp edges and richer pigment. All are carved with runes in ancient Draconic that flicker faintly when touched.

### Pillar 1: The Breath of the First Dawn
*Oldest, near-erased. Silver and white marble tiles faded to ivory.*

A radiant serpentine figure spirals from a disc of light in the sky (Bahamut in symbolic form), exhaling motes of flame, frost, storm, and shadow that fall like seeds into the world below. Around the base, four smaller wyrms coil in slumber, their red, blue, green, and gold scales barely visible beneath dust and age.

{secret}Meaning: The divine creation: the birth of dragons as embodiments of the world's elements.{/secret}

### Pillar 2: The Shaping of the Lands
*Early, fractured but vibrant blues and greens.*

Dragons of every hue soar across oceans and mountains, their breath shaping continents: a red dragon's fire melts new valleys, a white one freezes rivers, a bronze one calls lightning that splits the sky. Below, small figures (mortal races) build shrines and raise their hands in awe.

{secret}Meaning: Dragons once ruled as gods of creation and change, revered and feared equally.{/secret}

### Pillar 3: The Rival Thrones
*Mid-aged; fiery red tones dominate.*

Two immense dragons, one silver, one crimson, lock claws midair above a burning plain. Around them, lesser dragons clash in storms of elemental fury while mortals flee below. Between them hangs a shattered crown of stone.

{secret}Meaning: The Age of Dragon Wars: brotherhood turning to dominion, the gods' children fighting for supremacy.{/secret}

### Pillar 4: The Celestial Judgment
*Gilded edges, faint celestial motifs.*

A radiant humanoid silhouette, possibly Bahamut, looms in the heavens above kneeling dragons. Light cascades down, fracturing into seven beams, each touching a dragon of a different color. One turns away in pride while the others bow.

{secret}Meaning: The first divine rift: pride separating dragonkind from their maker.{/secret}

### Pillar 5: The Age of Flame and Shadow
*Dark and oppressive; blacks and reds dominate.*

Humanoids toil beneath towering dragons, dragging chains of gold and heaps of gems toward colossal draconic thrones. One panel shows a mother shielding her child from a dragon's gaze as fire scorches the horizon. Above, Bahamut's sigil, cracked and dim, watches in silence, a single silver tear falling. A cruel red dragon sits enthroned, claws dripping with molten coin, grinning.

{secret}Meaning: The age of fear: mortals enslaved, lives reduced to tribute; the gods dismayed by their children's tyranny.{/secret}

### Pillar 6: The Betrayer's Blade
*Sharper detail, newer craft, darker tones.*

A shadowy humanoid figure stands atop a mountain of dragon corpses, its features indistinct. Its blade drips with red and black glass shards. The dragons above writhe in agony, wings torn, hearts pierced by light that looks like binding chains.

{secret}Meaning: The unknown destroyer: perhaps Vhallion's order, or the first dragon slayer. Intentionally ambiguous.{/secret}

### Pillar 7: The Fall of Fire
*Grim and visceral; fresh color, as if recently restored.*

Countless mortal armies swarm a battlefield of flame and ruin. Siege engines hurl chains wreathed in light; ballistae pierce wings; wizards rain spells like arrows. At the center lies a dying red dragon, vast and majestic even in death, scales cracked and smoking. Mortals cheer upon its corpse while a clutch of hatchlings huddles beneath one broken wing, eyes glowing with fear and hatred.

{secret}Meaning: The annihilation of dragonkind; mortal triumph shadowed by tragedy.{/secret}

### Pillar 8: The Shattered Pantheon
*Cracked, jagged art; pieces missing or melted.*

Temples collapse, draconic idols are desecrated, and Bahamut's symbol is darkened. Magma seeps from the cracks as if the carving bleeds. A single silver eye watches from the void, tears of molten light falling into ruin.

{secret}Meaning: The death of faith: Bahamut's temples abandoned, dragons' extinction nearly complete.{/secret}

### Pillar 9: The Egg and the Grove
*Serene mosaic; natural greens and silvers, unusually peaceful.*

Five dragon eggs, each a different color, rest within the roots of a massive tree in a tranquil grove. Tiny runes in the bark shimmer faintly; translated, they give the coordinates or features of an unfamiliar plane.

{secret}Meaning: Hope reborn: the dragons' legacy hidden, awaiting a time of unity (a subtle hint to where the dragons went).{/secret}

### Pillar 10: The Mocking Sovereign
*Newer than the others, but chipped crudely, as if made in spite.*

A skeletal figure stands triumphant atop the same dragon's remains, robes flaring in spectral fire, holding aloft a goblet carved from a dragon's fang and pouring molten gold over the corpse in mock offering. Lesser mages applaud in the background, raising scrolls and banners. Along the bottom, in fresh, cutting script: "Even gods bleed. Even dragons kneel."

{secret}Meaning: The victor's mockery: Vhallion's desecration of the dragon's memory, and Bahamut's silence.{/secret}
