## Read-Aloud
You lie in your bunks, gear nearby but not at hand, resting in the fragile peace of a job well done. The embers in the hearth have burned low, casting long shadows that flicker with every gust that sneaks through the old shutters.

Then: **a scream.** High-pitched. Desperate. It rips you from sleep like a blade drawn across a taut string. There's a crash, wood splintering, glass shattering, and then a shriek so guttural and alien it seems to come from inside your own skull.

Then a mother's voice, frantic and breaking: *"Stay behind me! I just need the keys, just stay back!"*

Through the main hall, dimly lit by moonlight seeping through warped boards, you see her: a woman in torn clothing, frantically pawing through a satchel dropped by the door. Beside her, a small boy holds a lit torch, shaking violently. His wide eyes shimmer with tears as he screams, *"Mom! Run!"*

*CRASH!* From the shadows behind them, something monstrous drops from the thatching. It lands with unnatural grace, claws gouging the gaps in the stone path as it straightens, its sinewy frame blotting out the moonlight: a twisted, pale creature, leathery wing-like fringe stretching from its arms, muscles taut beneath bristling fur. Its mouth hisses open too wide, its enlarged fangs dripping with fresh blood. Glowing red eyes scan the street, and fixate on the child.

The creature lunges, but the boy's trembling torch flares and catches its outstretched hand. It screeches and reels back, smoke curling from where the flame kissed its corrupted flesh. It backs up, crawling along the wall like a lizard, head cocked, calculating... waiting.

The boy stands his ground, sobbing and screaming for help, his arm shaking under the torch's weight. His mother lies pinned beneath fallen furniture, conscious but bleeding, struggling to reach her son.

The beast stalks just out of reach of the flame, pacing on the wall above the boy like a wolf circling a trapped rabbit. He is moments from death, and you are all that stands in its way.

**What do you do?**
