## Overview
Founded by former members of the [[Unsighted Order]] who believed in a balanced approach to magic, the Wardens seek to maintain harmony between magic and mundane existence. They guard ancient knowledge and artifacts, strive to prevent the misuse of magic, and advocate coexistence and understanding between magical and non-magical communities. {secret}They oppose the [[Obsidian Order]]'s schemes to preserve magical equilibrium.{/secret}
