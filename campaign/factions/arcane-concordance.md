## Overview
The Arcane Concordance began as a refuge for those afflicted by [[The Weave|the weave]]: nomadic mages marginalized by society for their abilities. It splintered from the [[Unsighted Order]] and grew into a structured university under Archmage [[Faythe Kazula]], gaining a prestigious reputation for research, teaching, and magical innovation and attracting talent from across the realm.

## Structure
- **Main campus and satellites:** the university centers on a main campus overseen by the Archmage, with satellite campuses and research centers headed by Grand Mages, who form a council advising the Archmage. Campuses sit near the ancient [[Obelisks]], which suppress raw magic and amplify those who learn to harness them.
- **Education:** students spend about six years attending daily lectures, choosing specialties, and working with mentors and academic advisors.
- **The trial:** a closely guarded, week-long final trial (see [[Ameliorate]]).
- **Integration into society:** graduates, the [[Ameliorate]], are trusted, productive members of society, encouraged to use their skills for the betterment of their communities.

## Philosophy
The Concordance prides itself on ethical magical practice, innovation, and preparing mages to wield power responsibly, combining rigorous academics with practical trials and upholding a tradition of excellence and secrecy in its methods.

## Faythe's Hidden Agenda {secret}
Unbeknownst to most, Faythe believes magic users have been historically oppressed and feared by non-magical societies, and that they must be made to atone.

1. **Expansion and influence:** using the university's reputation as a facade, Faythe deploys graduates and trusted agents as spies across the realm. They infiltrate courts, noble houses, religious institutions, and military commands, gathering intelligence and manipulating events to sow discord and weaken existing regimes.
2. **Agenda of retribution:** a harsh reckoning in which non-magical people atone for past transgressions, through forced servitude, exile, or worse depending on the perceived injustice.
3. **Magical supremacy:** a hierarchy where magic users hold sway over every part of society, magic is openly revered, and the magically talented are recognized as natural leaders.

**Methods:** subterfuge, political manipulation, and psychological warfare, without drawing undue attention.

**Ethical dilemmas:** many members are unaware of Faythe's true intentions and believe in the noble pursuit of magical knowledge. Those who find out must grapple with loyalty, ethics, and the greater good.

**Resistance:** as her plans unfold, resistance may come both from magic users wary of her extreme methods and from non-magic users protecting their rights against magical tyranny.
