## Overview
A revered order of monks dedicated to upholding justice, protecting the innocent, and striving for personal enlightenment. It is divided into factions, each led by a Grandmaster who is a paragon of that faction's expertise. All unite under a common goal of goodness and selfless service.

## Factions
1. [[Faction of the Vigilant Sentinels]]: martial arts and combat
2. [[Faction of the Benevolent Healers]]: healing and medicine
3. [[Faction of the Enlightened Scholars]]: scholarship and philosophy
4. [[Faction of the Harmonious Sages]]: diplomacy, meditation, conflict resolution
5. [[Faction of the Elemental Mystics]]: elemental and natural magic
6. [[Faction of the Astral Seers]]: divination, prophecy, cosmic insight

## Grandmaster Council
The highest governing body: all current Grandmasters. They convene on major decisions affecting the whole Order, putting critical issues to a democratic vote after thorough discussion.

## Succession
To become a Grandmaster, a monk must challenge the current Grandmaster of their chosen faction, in that Grandmaster's expertise. For example:
- Sentinels: a duel of martial prowess.
- Healers: treating the most critical patients.
- Scholars: a debate or a complex philosophical dilemma.
- Sages: mediating a significant dispute or negotiating a delicate peace.

## Core Values
- Protecting the innocent and vulnerable.
- Providing aid and sustenance to those in need.
- Seeking personal enlightenment and spiritual growth.
- Fostering harmony within the Order and the world.

## Activities
- Running shelters and soup kitchens for the homeless and destitute.
- Defending villages and towns from marauders and monsters.
- Research and study into the world and the self.
- Serving as mediators and peacemakers.

## Symbol
A serene meditative figure surrounded by gentle ripples: peace, enlightenment, and the ripple effect of good deeds spreading through the world.
