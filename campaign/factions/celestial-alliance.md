## Overview
Founded in the [[Reformation Era]] amid rising magical instability and extra-planar threats, the Celestial Alliance investigates the disappearance of magical beasts, political instability in established governments, and the emergence of extra-planar creatures. It promotes peace, safeguards civilizations, and provides humanitarian aid in times of crisis.

## Leadership
At the forefront stands a revered aasimar, [[Tarren Milwyn]], seen by the public as the embodiment of hope and virtue. Tarren heads the organization, rallying support and fostering unity among diverse factions and nations.

{secret}Behind the scenes, true leadership rests with an ancient and powerful deity or creature of immense celestial power and wisdom. Its presence is veiled to prevent undue reverence or discord, but its influence shapes the Alliance's strategy at the highest levels.{/secret}

## Mission and Activities
1. **Investigating mysteries:** investigators, diplomats, and scholars explore ruins, study texts, and work with magical experts to find the causes of the vanishing magical beasts and the influx of extra-planar creatures.
2. **Humanitarian aid:** refugee camps, supplies, and medical help for regions hit by political instability, disasters, or magical threats.
3. **Political engagement:** diplomacy with governments and factions, advocating stability, protection of magical beings, and cooperation.

## Challenges
- **Secrecy:** balancing its public image against hiding its celestial leadership.
- **Intrigue:** resistance from secretive factions, rogue mages, and those exploiting chaos.
- **Ethical dilemmas:** the line between proactive protection and respecting mortal free will.
