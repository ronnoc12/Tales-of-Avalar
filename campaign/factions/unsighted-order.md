## Overview
Amid the persecution of the [[Era of Darkness]], a nomadic group of marked mages, led by a visionary whose name is now lost to history, founded the Unsighted Order. Its mission was to find a way for mages to harness and control their chaotic energies for positive purposes.

They discovered that the ancient [[Obelisks]] could tame wild magic ([[Discovery of the Obelisks]]), laying the foundation for a safer world for magic users and beginning the [[Reformation Era]].

## Today
The Unsighted Order still exists in its own right. The [[Arcane Concordance]] splintered from it, and former members founded the [[Wardens of Ethereal Balance]].

## Vhallion's Cult {secret}
The Unsighted Order is devoted to the lich [[Vhallion]].
- **The box:** the Order arranged for the box binding Vhallion's soul to be given to [[King Alaric III]] as a diplomat's gift. [[Sorcerer-Lord Velmion Drex]] orchestrated it and [[Thorn of Glass]] delivered it. When Alaric opened it, Vhallion escaped into him.
- **The phylactery:** the Order guards Vhallion's phylactery in the [[Ashvault Sanctum]]. They used old, forbidden magic to bring a horrifying creature there to protect it, and maintain modern wards that keep intruders out and the monster in.
- **Monsters:** the Order creates mutated monsters, and Vhallion's Arcane Registry gives them lists of magically gifted people to use for even more powerful ones.
- **Elites:** "Unsighted Order elites" defend Vhallion's interests.
