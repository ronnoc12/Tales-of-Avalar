## Overview
A decentralized network of small, organized branches spread across the realm, all answering to a central governing body. USE connects clients with skilled individuals or teams for tasks from the mundane to the extraordinary.

## Organization
1. **Central Council:** elder members with specialized knowledge set policy, resolve disputes, and oversee major operations.
2. **Regional branches:** led by experienced guildmasters who recruit, train, and manage operatives. The [[Midcross#USE Midcross Outpost|Midcross branch]] is led by [[Casfer Ferndance]]; the headquarters in [[Valoria]] is run by his twin, [[Cassie Ferndance]].
3. **Operatives:** skills range from espionage, assassination, and diplomacy to magic and combat. Every operative is vetted and trained to the guild's standards of professionalism and secrecy.

## Services
- **Contract jobs:** espionage, artifact retrieval, protection, or sabotage.
- **Consultation:** security measures, strategic planning, and magical defenses.
- **Networking:** members build skills and contacts with other professionals.

## Code of Conduct
- **Secrecy:** absolute confidentiality about missions and clients.
- **Professionalism:** members conduct themselves with professionalism and integrity.
- **Neutrality:** USE stays neutral in political conflicts so it can work across factions and kingdoms.

## Reputation
Renowned for reliability, discretion, and handling complex tasks, and feared by some for its influence and the consequences of crossing its operatives. USE keeps relationships with nobility, merchants, and influential figures, ensuring a steady flow of contracts: a shadowy yet indispensable force for anyone with coin and a need for discreet action.
