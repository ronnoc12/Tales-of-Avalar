## Overview
Enigmatic magical sentinels who patrol the borders and alleys of [[Akhram's Oasis]], loyal only to [[Thena]] and her vision of the Oasis as a bastion of independence and power. They keep watch on those who delve too deep into forbidden magic, and intervene quickly when wild magic threatens the city's peace.

*"Watch out for the Teeth, you never know what they hear."*

## Origin {secret}
The Guardians were crafted through a pact with [[Faythe Kazula]], founder of the [[Arcane Concordance]]. Thena is unaware of Faythe's grand designs.
