## Overview
Formerly the Order of Nullification, The Enlightened is a zealous organization dedicated to eradicating magic from the world. They see magic as a corrupting influence that defies divine order and threatens civilization, and they persecute its practitioners as heretics. They rebranded as The Enlightened during the [[Reformation Era]] and hold their stronghold in [[Sanctum]], though dissent within their ranks threatens to reignite old conflicts.

## Ideology
True enlightenment and purity come only through rejecting magic and embracing divine will. They cite scriptures and prophecies denouncing magic as an affront to the natural order and a tool of deception and chaos.

## Structure
1. **Grand Inquisitor:** the supreme authority on doctrine and enforcement; directs the regional Chapters and campaigns against magic users.
2. **Chapters and Inquisitors:** regional Chapters led by High Inquisitors operate autonomously under central doctrine. Inquisitors are trained in investigation, interrogation, and combat against magical threats.
3. **Divine justification:** they interpret ancient texts as divine mandate and work closely with orthodox religious institutions to enforce anti-magic laws.

## Tactics
- **Interrogation and torture** to extract confessions and identify accomplices: waterboarding, thumbscrews, prolonged psychological manipulation.
- **Infiltration and informants** in communities suspected of harboring magic users.
- **Public spectacles:** high-profile trials and executions, such as burning at the stake or parading the convicted through town squares.
- **Anti-magic artifacts and wards:** enchanted weapons and wards that nullify magical abilities.

## Impact
They rule through fear and coercion, terrorizing magical communities and dissuading others from practicing the arcane. Their campaigns breed paranoia and division as neighbors turn on suspected magic users to avoid scrutiny.

## Opposition
- **Resistance movements:** underground networks of magic users and sympathizers wage guerrilla resistance and sabotage.
- **Ethical criticism:** critics decry their methods as barbaric and unjust.
