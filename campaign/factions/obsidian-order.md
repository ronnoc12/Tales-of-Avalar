## Overview
A secretive underground organization that studies and exploits magical myths, monsters, and ancient legends as sources of power. Led by a cabal known only as the Inner Circle, it works with ruthless determination to amass arcane knowledge and artifacts, believing true power lies in controlling the mystical forces that shape the world. Unlike other factions, it seeks dominance, not coexistence.

## Methods {secret}
1. **Arcane research and experimentation:** extensive research into ancient texts, artifacts, and magical creatures, often by morally dubious methods.
2. **Artifact acquisition:** skilled operatives acquire relics and enchanted items to study, reverse-engineer, or weaponize.
3. **Summoning and control of creatures:** mythical beasts bound through rituals serve as guardians, soldiers, or tools of intimidation.
4. **Infiltration and subversion:** operatives infiltrate governments, noble houses, and organizations, manipulating events and sowing discord.

## Structure {secret}
1. **Inner Circle:** the highest-ranking members; they set strategy and wield deep arcane lore and significant magic.
2. **Sorcerer-Lords:** powerful magic users overseeing regional operations, field ops, and experimental labs, each commanding loyal disciples.
3. **Operatives and scholars:** researchers, arcane engineers, and field agents chosen for their expertise in magic, combat, or espionage.

## The Parasitic Organism {secret}
Through bio-magical experimentation, the Order created a genetically modified parasite, inspired by illithid larvae, that infiltrates and controls the minds of magical creatures and beasts, bending them to the will of those the leadership deems worthy. It is tailored to target specific magical signatures and vulnerabilities.

**The unknown vulnerability:** in field tests, some infected creatures showed sporadic bursts of uncontrollable aggression or erratic behavior, briefly resisting their controllers. Unresolved, this risks:
- **Backlash:** creatures turning on handlers or causing collateral damage that exposes the Order.
- **Loss of control:** secrets exposed to rivals, or retaliatory strikes.
- **Internal dissent:** the morality of enslaving sentient beings could fracture the Order.

Sorcerer-Lords, researchers, and dark alchemists are racing to correct the flaw.

## In the Hollow Quarter {secret}
Beneath [[Valoria]]'s undercity, the Order tests its parasites on magical beasts, abducts or recruits self-taught mages, and uses the chaos as a research field. Most residents know only vague rumors or manipulated benefactors. Mysterious "benefactors" fund Hollow Quarter research, and the researchers vanish once their work matures.

## Challenges
- **Ethical opposition** from ethical magic users, religious groups, and humanitarian organizations.
- **Rival factions,** including the [[Arcane Concordance]] and the [[Wardens of Ethereal Balance]].
- **Internal strife:** ambitious members vie for power, risking betrayal.
