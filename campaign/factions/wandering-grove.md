## Origins and Cultural Resilience
In the turbulent aftermath of [[The Sundering]] and the persecution of magic users, a group of nomadic mages emerged to forge their own destiny. Culturally they resemble the Romani of our world, but with a distinctly magical twist. Most studied natural magic, such as druids, or are people whose connection to the weave manifested as wild magic: unpredictable and untamed.

## Chaos and Control
At first their abilities were seen as pure chaos, capable of effects deemed impossible by modern magic and incantations, and dangerous to wielders and bystanders alike. Over generations of honing their skills and deepening their bond with nature, aided by powerful artifacts and ancient knowledge, they learned to control their abilities through rituals, meditation, and communal practice.

They also train mages in **Aramente**, a program found only among the Wanderers and the high druids of [[Lei-aluran]].

## Nomadic Life
Many still embrace a nomadic lifestyle, moving with the seasons and the rhythms of the world. They travel in close-knit caravans adorned with protective charms and symbols of their heritage, known for resilience, resourcefulness, and bonds forged through shared struggle.

## Traditions and the Grove
They preserve ancient traditions through oral histories and mystical lore, and hold sacred the knowledge of the Wandering Grove: a mystical forest said to move across the land, offering sanctuary and spiritual guidance to those attuned to it. Ritual dances, storytelling, and crafting magical artifacts serve both practical and spiritual purposes.

## Persecution and Acceptance
Despite their contributions, they often face prejudice from settled communities, and their reputation as unpredictable outsiders has barred them from many cities. Among those who value their wisdom, they find allies and kindred spirits.

## Recent Events
The goblins of [[Vargeez]]'s tribe, starving after fleeing south, were taken in by the Wanderers, who shared food and taught them to hunt and trap. Many of the goblins now travel with the Grove.

## Legacy
In a new era of magical reformation and political intrigue, the Wanderers stand as a testament to resilience and cultural preservation, and their quest for harmony between wild magic and civilization offers hope for coexistence.
