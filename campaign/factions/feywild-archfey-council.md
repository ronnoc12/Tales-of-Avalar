## Overview
The archfey of the [[Feywild]]: [[Lady Morivelle]], [[Nyxylia]], [[Rhiannon]], and [[Toremona]]. Helping one archfey can have unintended consequences for another; performing a favor for Morivelle, for example, might expose Toremona to a minor theft of dreams.

{secret}[[Lyzandral]], the Mother of Monsters, works within and around the Court, manipulating it from the shadows.{/secret}

## Crystal Trade
- Only a few [[Aether Crystals|aether crystals]] exist in the Feywild, and each archfey negotiates or tests the party according to their nature.
- Cleverness, bravery, and emotional sincerity matter; brute force rarely succeeds.
- The crystals stabilize the party's flow of time. Without one, even a few miles in the Feywild could cost weeks, months, or years on the Material Plane.
