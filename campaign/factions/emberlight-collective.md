## Overview
A decentralized, grassroots movement of teachers, tinkerers, and guardians in [[Valoria]]'s Hollow Quarter. The Collective doesn't seek to destroy Valoria, but to hold it accountable and make sure its ideals reach everyone.

## Activities
- Running secret learning halls and invention spaces.
- Archiving and protecting cultural traditions and folk magics.
- Smuggling arcane textbooks and blueprints into poor communities.

## Leadership
[[Professor Telra Yenn]], a former university arcanist exiled after a magical accident, coordinates the safehouses and a network of underground schools.

## Conflicts
- Regular clashes with overzealous city regulators.
- {secret}Unknowingly infiltrated or manipulated by [[Obsidian Order]] agents posing as generous patrons.{/secret}
- Pressure from noble interests who want to "clean up" the Hollow Quarter.
