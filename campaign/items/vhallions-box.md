## Overview
Presented to [[King Alaric III]] as a "symbol of eternal stability," a gift from a foreign diplomat. It was arranged by the [[Unsighted Order]] and delivered by [[Thorn of Glass]].

## What It Was
It bound [[Vhallion]]'s soul to the soul of a guardian entity. Vhallion tortured the guardian until it willingly moved on, weakening the prison; when Alaric opened the box, Vhallion escaped into him.

## Rebinding
Another soul sacrificed to guard Vhallion could rebind him to the box: one of the three ways to free Alaric. Part of [[Nyss Kettlecrank]]'s stolen dimensional-capacitor design was used in it.
