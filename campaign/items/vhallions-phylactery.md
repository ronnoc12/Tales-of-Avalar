## Overview
[[Vhallion]]'s phylactery, crafted with dragon's blood, separate from the box and long lost to time. The [[Unsighted Order]] guards it in the [[Ashvault Sanctum]]. Destroying it is one of the three ways to free [[King Alaric III]].

## Where It Lies
Hidden inside the ribcage of the slain dragon [[Vortheryx]], sealed within a crystalline heartstone: a dead, blackened flame crystal that only glows when touched by someone carrying "a dragon's spark."

## Protections
- Illusion wards.
- A mind-lock puzzle in Draconic.
- A curse that makes intruders feel their flesh is turning to scales and being ripped off.
- The Sanctum's guardian (see [[Reverse Medusa Guardian]]).

## Fate
The previous party recovered it from the [[Ashvault Sanctum]] but vanished before finishing their quest; they had done enough that the fey took over after their disappearance and destroyed it using the very material it was crafted from: dragon's blood.
