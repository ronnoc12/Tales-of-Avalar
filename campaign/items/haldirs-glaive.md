## Overview
The legendary glaive once wielded by [[Haldir]]. Since his death it lies dormant, and its fate is unresolved.

{prep}Decide the glaive's current location, its stats, and what wakes it.{/prep}

## Nature {secret}
The glaive holds a single bitter wyrm-soul that has learned to perpetuate itself unnaturally by consuming dragon souls. It spoke to Haldir and grew stronger with each soul it consumed.

## The Soul War {secret}
The glaive recognizes the [[Tome of Dragon Memory]] as its mortal enemy. When Haldir first saw the tome, the glaive erupted in psychic agony and burned a single command into his mind: **"UNMAKE."**

- **The tome** embodies the collective memory of dragonkind, countless voices united, and is currently far stronger.
- **The glaive** is one soul, but it can rival the tome if it keeps feeding: at 0-1 souls the tome dominates; at 2-3 the glaive gains a foothold; at 4-5 they are equal; at 6+ the glaive outgrows the tome, which then fights for survival.
- A wielder who attunes to both becomes the tether between them. Feeding the glaive could eventually produce an artifact that usurps the tome itself.
