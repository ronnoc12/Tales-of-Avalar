## Overview
A vast, mysterious artifact of immense magical power, said to have been created during the war between the [[Sylvani]] and the [[Charr]] in the [[Age of Primordial Chaos]], and the key to both races' fall. It was the focal point of magical and planar energy: a conduit that kept the veil between the planes thin.

## Fate
It shattered under the weight of a cosmic struggle ([[The Shattering]]). The veils thickened, its essence scattered across Avalar as dangerous relics, strange phenomena, and magical scars, and its shards became the [[Obelisks]].
