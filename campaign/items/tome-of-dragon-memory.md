## Description
A weighty tome bound in petrified dragon scales. Silver fire dances through veins in its pages, alive with the memories of dragons long dead, written in Draconic, a language so rare that only dedicated scholars or ancient bloodlines can read it. It does not merely record knowledge; it binds a soul to its will.

Found in the [[Ashvault Sanctum]] on a pedestal of black stone beside the slain dragon [[Vortheryx]].

{prep}Who holds or is attuned to the tome now (Haldir was attempting attunement before his death)?{/prep}

## Contents
- Dragon lifecycle and soulbinding rituals.
- Draconic philosophy: "Fire is not destruction; it is memory set alight."
- A prophecy warning of a "dead sun wrapped in words" {secret}(a veiled reference to [[Vhallion]]){/secret}.
- Names of dragons that survived the purge, with coded locations.

## Attunement
Attuning takes 1 hour and permanently binds your soul to the tome. Only *Wish*, divine intervention, or the tome's destruction can sever the bond. The tome is harmless unless you act against its will. A false heart may be rejected outright, with psychic backlash, and perhaps a draconic "traitor" mark that future dragons would recognize.

## Properties (While Attuned)
- **Draconic Tongue.** You can read, speak, and write Draconic.
- **Echo of Memory.** Once per long rest, the tome reveals a draconic truth as a vision; this works like *Legend Lore*, limited to dragons, their culture, their enemies, or their extinction.
- **Draconic Resilience.** Resistance to one dragon damage type (fire, cold, lightning, acid, or poison), chosen by the DM at attunement.
- **Voice of Authority.** Proficiency (or expertise, if already proficient) in Intimidation and Persuasion with creatures who fear, revere, or know the truth of dragons.

## Drawbacks
- **Unshakable Bond.** If you knowingly act against a dragon's survival, make a Charisma save (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + your level). On a failure, the action is canceled and you take psychic damage equal to your level. After three failures, the tome fractures your soul: you permanently lose 2d6 maximum hit points.
- **Moral Weight.** When a choice involves a dragon's fate, succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom save or be compelled to argue for sparing or freeing it.
- **Soulbound.** If the tome is destroyed while you are attuned, you lose one character level and take 4d10 psychic damage that cannot be reduced or prevented.
- **Sentience.** If you deceive it or use it selfishly, it may contest your will with Charisma saves; on a failure it seizes control of your body for one action or imposes disadvantage on your next roll.

## Destroying the Tome
Only in the fire of a living dragon's breath, while its bearer willingly releases their attunement.

## The Echo of Memory Vision {secret}
Its first vision points toward [[Akhram's Oasis]] without naming it, and reveals that Vhallion led the dragon genocide:

> Your breath stills as the tome pulses in your hands. The world around you melts into searing light and endless dunes. A figure of fire and shadow towers upon a dune crest, its eyes like coals that see through ages. Behind it, great wings blot out the sun, dozens, then hundreds, as a host of dragons wheels above the desert sky.
>
> One by one, those wings falter. Spears of flame and chains of shadow lash upward, dragging the dragons from the sky. Their roars become thunder; their blood, molten stars that rain across the sands. The towering figure laughs, and with each laugh another dragon falls.
>
> The vision fractures, leaving only the dunes. A spiraling rock formation rises from the sand, twisted like a frozen whirlwind. Beneath it yawns a dark fissure, pulsing with heat. You feel the weight of the storm around it, not yet born but waiting. Then the sands surge forward, consuming all, and you awaken with the taste of iron and ash on your tongue.

The spiraling rock and fissure are a hidden entrance, long since swallowed by the city that grew there.

## Tome vs. Glaive {secret}
The [[Haldir's Glaive|glaive]] sees the tome as its mortal enemy (see the glaive's entry). An attunement attempt by a glaive-bearer: Charisma save DC 18. Success: accepted, and the Soul War begins (each dawn roll d20: 1-13 tome dominates, 14-17 glaive whispers, 18-19 glaive asserts violently, 20 full psychic battle and a Charisma save or brief possession). Failure by less than 5: rejected until the glaive is weakened or the soul strengthened. Failure by 5 or more: 4d6 psychic damage, stunned until the end of the next turn, and the tome views the bearer as compromised.
