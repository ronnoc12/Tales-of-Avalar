## Overview
A small, secluded lumber-milling town nestled deep within a dense forest known for its unique black-tinted sap trees.

## Location and Geography
Ravenwood is surrounded by towering trees that produce a distinct black sap. Dense forests, rolling hills, and a tranquil, almost mystical atmosphere have contributed to its isolation. It sits between two mountains, which made access challenging for most of its history, though better roads and demand for its sap and lumber have made it much easier to reach.

## Economy
- **Lumber milling:** the town harvests and processes the black-sap trees. The sap gives the wood a distinctive ashy black color with dark rings, highly sought after for its look and unique properties.
- **Employment:** most residents work for the local milling company, which dominates the economy and keeps the town fed and running.

## Culture and Society
- **Xenophobia and isolation:** the townspeople are wary of outsiders, preferring their traditional way of life, rooted in a fear of losing their livelihoods or the sanctity of their surroundings.
- **Southern influence:** a rural, southern ethos: hard work, family bonds, and community solidarity. Traditional southern hospitality coexists with caution toward strangers.

## Politics
- **Governance:** the mayor, [[Logan Porter]], embodies the town's conservative values and prioritizes economic stability over outside influence or modernization.
- **Community relations:** close-knit, but tensions simmer under economic uncertainty and fear of outside interference. Environmental protests led by figures like [[Blarney Blene]] have stirred unrest and highlighted divisions.

## The Black Sap Trees
Prized for their timber, the trees also hold cultural significance; some believe they have mystical properties or ties to local folklore. Concerns about deforestation, wildlife habitats, and the sustainability of the trees have sparked debate about balancing economic needs with ecological responsibility.
