## Overview
The fortified bastion and ideological heart of [[The Enlightened]], designed to embody their vision of a world cleansed of magic and governed by divine order.

## History
Sanctum is a surviving city of the [[Human City-States]] of the [[Classical Era]]. Its clerics were even more prevalent before [[The Sundering]].

## Governing Structure
The Grand Inquisitor, supreme authority within The Enlightened, oversees the city. Below them, appointed High Judges each oversee part of the city's administration, justice, and defense. Strict religious doctrine and anti-magic law are enforced by a vigilant network of Inquisitors, guards, and informants.

## Life in Sanctum
- **Architecture:** imposing stone walls, towering spires covered in religious iconography, and labyrinthine streets designed to discourage infiltration and give ample surveillance points. Public squares are dominated by statues of the cause's martyrs and heroes.
- **Daily rituals:** prayers, sermons, and communal meals in grand cathedrals dedicated to divine entities believed to oppose magic.
- **Social hierarchy:** standing depends on loyalty to The Enlightened and adherence to anti-magic law. Officials, devout clergy, and artisans who serve the city's defense are esteemed; suspected magic users and dissidents are ostracized, imprisoned, or worse.
- **Education and indoctrination:** clergy-run schools teach obedience to divine will, rejection of magic, and sacrifice and service.
- **Economy and trade:** self-sufficient (agriculture, craftsmanship, resource extraction). Trade is strictly regulated to keep out arcane artifacts and heretical ideas. God relics and clerical or divine magic items are exempt.

## Challenges and Tensions
- **Resistance movements:** underground networks of magic users and sympathizers secretly oppose the regime and preserve magical knowledge.
- **Political intrigue:** rival factions use zealotry and ambition to challenge the Grand Inquisitor and the High Judges.
- **External threats:** its isolation and stance make it a target for neighboring realms and magical entities, some eager to seize its troves of religious artifacts and knowledge.
