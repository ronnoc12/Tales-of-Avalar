## Location
A small but formidable city on a rugged coastline of harsh winds and towering cliffs, built into the side of a great mountain with stone structures hewn from the rock. Its natural defenses make it hard to invade: a fortress for its isolationist beliefs.

## Government
The Council of the Iron Order (heads of important guilds, military commanders, and elder citizens) keeps the city self-sufficient and secure. The High Steward position rotates among council members every five years and leads with discipline, personal responsibility, and restraint.

## Magic and Control
Magic is heavily regulated. The Wardens of the Iron Seal, a special order of magical enforcers, keep magic within what is considered necessary or useful; unauthorized magic is punished severely.

## Culture
- Self-reliant people who value hard work, physical strength, and pragmatic solutions; magic is seen as a crutch or a dangerous indulgence.
- Festivals and ceremonies celebrate strength of will and character: fortitude over comfort.
- Citizens are raised with an aversion to magic, associated with weakness and dependency.

## Economy
- **Self-sufficient:** excellent crafting, especially weapons, armor, and sturdy goods; the harsh environment has driven innovation in metallurgy.
- **Isolationist trade:** a limited but solid network in stone, metal, and crafted weapons. Relations with magic-heavy places are cautious and avoid magical goods.
