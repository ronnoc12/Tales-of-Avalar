## Overview
The plane of the Fae. During the [[Extermination of the Dragons]], many dragons fled here; some integrated with the Fae, while the chromatic dragons who tried to seize Fae territory were driven out or slain at great cost. The Feywild remains independent.

## True Names
The fey guard their true names closely and go by monikers. A true name may be shared in the gravest of bonds, such as wedding vows.

## Politics
The Feywild is held by the [[Feywild Archfey Council|archfey]]: [[Lady Morivelle]], [[Nyxylia]], [[Rhiannon]], and [[Toremona]]. {secret}[[Lyzandral]], the Mother of Monsters, manipulates them from the shadows.{/secret}

## Time
Time flows unpredictably here. Without an [[Aether Crystals|aether crystal]] to stabilize it, traveling even a few miles could cost weeks, months, or years on the Material Plane.

## Places
### Crimson Vault {vault}
Guarded by [[Nyxylia]], High Sovereign of Defense. It holds dragon blood. {secret}Among it is a fragment of the blood of the black dragon Vhallion sent, now Lyzandral's agent: leverage she doesn't know exists.{/secret}

### Ossuary Wilds {realm}
[[Lyzandral]]'s realm: a labyrinthine, mutable forest of organic horrors, glowing fungi, and rivers of black, viscous water. Trees subtly shift their positions, roots whisper secrets, and grotesque, semi-intelligent creatures stalk the shadows.
