## Location
Deep within an ancient mountain range, sheltered by towering cliffs, beside its sister city [[BrokRogGnakShragBromKur]].

## History
The kobolds once lived in sprawling swamplands beneath the mountains. When a great flood, caused by melting ancient ice during a time the kobolds call the Great Thaw, drove them into the mountains, they adapted quickly. They learned to thrive in low light, using magic and ingenuity to cultivate crops and sustain their community. Ancient draconic magic adds mysticism and power to their society.

## Government
[[Nesrith]] serves as figurehead leader. She governs hands-off through a council of kobolds, acting as mediator and tie-breaker. She could rule with more authority but believes in letting the kobolds govern themselves, fostering unity and self-reliance.

{secret}Nesrith appears to be a blue dragonborn but is in fact an adult blue dragon in disguise, deeply loyal to her people and rejecting the BBEG's manipulation of her family.{/secret}

## Politics
Under Nesrith, the kobolds are increasingly involved in BrokRogGnakShragBromKur's politics, supporting [[Tornus]]'s rebellion against the dual kings.

## Magic in Klag
- **Focus:** low-light magic (illumination, shadow manipulation) and water magic (irrigation, purification) to sustain the city's infrastructure and food supply.
- **Cooperation:** magic users work with kobold engineers and craftsmen on crops, resource gathering, and sustainability; magic is expected to serve practical, everyday life.
- Wild magic is used more freely than elsewhere, though the community encourages bringing it under control.
