## Read-Aloud
Your adventure begins in the city of Midcross. It's a settlement that is hard to get an accurate population count for, as it is fundamentally a transient place with an unusual amount of trade for a place its size. It holds true to its namesake, lying at a midpoint between the larger inland cities, the larger port cities, and the regional farming, lumbering, and other trade routes and work sites.

The city is equipped with towering docks for sky ships, massive shipping yards, warehouses, lavish hotels, cheap motels, storage facilities, safety deposit box locations, and anything else you can imagine that aids the management and distribution of goods across a large area. Most residents are working-class people: transients accompanying cargo from one place to another, or those who tend to the needs of people moving cargo through Midcross.

## Overview
Midcross is a bustling trade city at a strategic midpoint along major trade routes, a pivotal hub for commerce and exchange in the realm.

## Governance
[[Lord Burgenroot]] is a pragmatic leader who prioritizes economic prosperity and trade efficiency above all else. He rules with ultimate authority, supported by an advisory council ([[Midcross Council]]) of influential merchants, guild leaders, and trusted advisors. Decisions on policy, infrastructure, and economic initiatives are made collectively by the council, and a unanimous vote is required to remove him from power.

## Description of the City
- **Trade hub:** bustling markets, busy warehouses, and a diverse population of traders, merchants, and transient workers. Streets are lined with workshops, storefronts, and inns catering to travelers and merchants.
- **Architecture:** practicality with a touch of grandeur. Stone buildings with wooden accents dominate; the main thoroughfares are wide and well-maintained for heavy cart and caravan traffic.
- **Cultural melting pot:** a diverse population from many regions and cultures, influencing everything from cuisine to customs and the traditions celebrated through the year.
- **Security and order:** well-maintained by vigilant city guards and private security hired by merchants and guilds. Crime is relatively low within the city limits.

## Impact on the World
- **Economic:** Midcross's trade has a significant impact on regional and global economies; neighboring towns and kingdoms rely on its routes.
- **Cultural exchange:** ideas, traditions, and innovations from distant lands converge here, fostering tolerance among its inhabitants.
- **Political:** decisions made in Midcross can ripple across the realm, influencing trade policy, diplomacy, and even military alliances.

## Hooks
- Lord Burgenroot has posted a bounty to find out why neither the lumber nor the last several representatives he sent to the milling town of [[Ravenwood]] have returned. The town has gone eerily quiet.
