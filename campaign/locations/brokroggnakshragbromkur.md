## Overview
A surviving city of the old [[Dwarven Kingdoms]], deep within an ancient mountain range beside its sister city [[Klag]]. Its people are dwarves and duergar.

## History
The dwarves and duergar were once enslaved by massive white beasts that forced them to mine precious minerals and gems. The dwarven heroes [[Duldren HammerBorn]] and [[Durugar Rotgurn SoftFollow]] led a revolt against the beasts and seized control of the city.

{secret}The tale of their victory is built on lies. They were not the true slayers of the beasts: they killed many of the beasts' followers, but the true beast escaped. They then killed those who would speak the truth and took the city's wealth and power for themselves.{/secret}

## Government
Two dual kings, one of the HammerBorn line and one of the Rotgurn line, are constantly at odds, scheming to eliminate each other. Organized crime flourishes and the underworld holds real power; the kings tacitly approve criminal enterprises, feeding a thriving black market in precious metals, gems, and magic-infused artifacts.

## Political Significance
Together with Klag, the city is highly strategic thanks to its mineral wealth and technical expertise. The sister cities control critical trade routes and supply much of the realm's weapons, armor, and enchanted tools.

A rebellion led by [[Tornus]], the half-dwarf, half-duergar heir and son of two exiled leaders, threatens the current power structure. He seeks to unite the warring factions and end centuries of chaos. Klag's kobolds support him, the rebellion is gaining momentum, and both cities are on the verge of open conflict.

## Hooks
The party is caught between the dual kings and Tornus's rebellion. Tornus offers a tempting contract to help overthrow the rulers; the kings' agents try to recruit the party to crush the rebellion. The party must navigate the politics and moral dilemmas of this fractured society. {secret}Along the way they can discover Tornus's true heritage.{/secret}

## Magic in the City
- Magic is tied to craftsmanship: runecrafting and enchanting imbue items with permanent effects, and alchemists experiment with potions and magical metallurgy.
- Formal arcane training is rare; most mages are self-taught or trained in craft guilds.
- Rumors speak of underground magical societies, criminal groups, and black-market traders dealing in forbidden magic. They may offer "protection" to wild magic users, at the cost of heavy debt.
