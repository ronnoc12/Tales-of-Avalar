## Overview
A powerful empire known for expansionist policies and formidable magical capabilities: magical constructs and summoned otherworldly creatures. After losing the [[War of the Distant Shore]] to [[Valoria]], along with its prized artifacts and much of its military, it retreated to regroup within its borders.
