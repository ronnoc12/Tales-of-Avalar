## Overview
A once-sacred volcanic shrine where flame-born dragons communed with the leylines beneath the world. Now a grave of molten crystal and scorched bones: a monument to extinction and arrogance.

## The Site
- **Construction:** carved from magma-glass and flame crystal; the walls refract light like stained fire. The architecture is not humanoid: vast halls meant for beings that walked on all fours and stood three stories tall.
- **Design:** a collapsed spire plunges into the volcano's heart, now cooled and layered with dragonbone and crystallized ash. Lava runs like blood through veins of glowing rock.
- **Atmosphere:** echoes whisper in Draconic. Now and then a low, reverent hum builds with the volcanic pressure, as if the mountain is remembering.

## The Final Flame
This was the den of [[Vortheryx]], the Eclipsed Flame, a red wyrm philosopher-king who tried to rally the remaining dragons against their destroyer{secret}, [[Vhallion]]{/secret}. He was hunted, betrayed by a corrupted drakeblood envoy, and slain here. The destroyer's{secret} (Vhallion's){/secret} forces desecrated the temple with propaganda murals portraying dragons as tyrants and beasts, and left the corpse intentionally brutalized: the skull, cracked open and fused with a cursed iron spike, displayed at the sanctum's center. His final words are carved beneath it: *"They came not for our fire, but for our future."*

## The Guardians {secret}
The [[Unsighted Order]] used old, forbidden magic to bring a horrifying creature here to guard [[Vhallion's Phylactery]] ([[Reverse Medusa Guardian]]). They still visit to maintain the bindings: fresh, modern sigils on the wards keep intruders out and the monster in, suppressing it just enough to allow access, but they crack if damaged.

## Places
### The Entrance {chamber}
The approach is a jagged volcanic tunnel, its walls glassy and blackened as if frozen mid-eruption. The air is acrid, thick with the metallic tang of old blood and ash. Just inside, a stone monolith carved with angular wards flickers faintly with unnatural light. Its design is not ancient but modern, clear proof that someone tends this place. Every few moments the wardstone pulses, as though listening for intruders. Shadows cling stubbornly to the corners, and the tunnel slopes down into the dark like a throat swallowing you whole.

### The Collapsed Hall {chamber}
You emerge into a wide hall whose roof has half-collapsed, letting shafts of reddish glow from magma fissures crackling across the floor. The heat presses against your skin like an open forge, and the smell of brimstone burns your lungs. Pillars of flame-crystal rise unevenly from the ground, many shattered, their shards glowing faintly like coals. One lies toppled, snapped in half, blackened as though clawed by something enormous. The hiss of magma and the drip of molten stone echo like distant screams.

### The Dragon's Corpse Chamber {chamber}
The passage widens into a vast cavern that reeks of death and sorrow. At its center lies the colossal skeleton of a dragon, its bones scorched, shattered, and blackened by blade and spell alike. The skull leers with hollow sockets, the jaw broken open in a final, silent scream. Broken scales and half-buried weapons from an ancient slaughter litter the ground.

Near one ribcage rests a pedestal of black stone, incongruous against the ruin. Upon it lies a massive tome bound in cracked scales, its pages filled with elegant curling script in a dead language few living remember (the [[Tome of Dragon Memory]]). The silence is absolute, broken only by ash shifting underfoot. The very air seems to mourn, the weight of extinction pressing down on all who stand here.

### The Ritual Chamber {chamber}
This room feels wrong before you cross the threshold. The floor is carved into a great ritual circle, etched with old blood that never dried, its lines glowing a dull crimson. The stone is unnaturally cold, and every sound is muffled, as if swallowed.

A stone altar slick with dark, iron-reeking stains stands to one side. Jagged knives, bone flutes, and chalices carved from skulls lie scattered as if the cult fled mid-ritual. The walls are scrawled with feverish symbols, some gouged deep, some painted in soot and ash. The very stone seems to remember the screams offered here.

### The Phylactery Lair {chamber}
At last you reach the heart of the sanctum: an oval cavern whose walls gleam like molten glass. The ceiling disappears into shadow, and from somewhere above comes the faint echo of stone shifting against stone, as if something immense is coiled just out of sight.

At the center stands a pedestal of volcanic glass, cracked but unbroken, and atop it rests a blackened reliquary box. The air is oppressive, every breath heavy, each sound dampened by an unnatural weight. The shadows writhe along the walls, too slow and deliberate to be tricks of the torchlight.

As you step forward you notice them: half-buried in the walls, the faces of women twisted in agony, mouths frozen in eternal screams. Their stone eyes glimmer faintly, and one by one, they begin to turn toward you.
