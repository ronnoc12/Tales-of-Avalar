## Overview
The Kingdom of Valoria sits in the heart of the continent. Its central location makes it a hub for trade and cultural exchange, with well-established roads connecting it to neighboring kingdoms and distant realms.

## Governance
A hereditary monarchy, currently ruled by [[King Alaric III]]. The royal family traces its lineage to the founding of the kingdom, with a long history of diplomatic prowess and military strength. The monarchy is supported by a council of advisors ([[Royal Council of Valoria]]) who help govern the realm and shape its policies.

## Goals and Ambitions
To maintain stability and prosperity within its borders while expanding its influence abroad through diplomacy and strategic alliances. Economic development, technological advancement, and cultural enrichment are key priorities.

## Political Alignment
Pragmatic: it prioritizes its own interests while maintaining diplomatic relations with neighbors. It leans toward neutrality unless directly threatened, preferring to broker peace and trade agreements that benefit its economy and people.

## Culture
A rich tapestry of art, music, literature, and craftsmanship. Cities are adorned with grand architecture, bustling marketplaces, and centers of learning such as universities and libraries. Annual festivals celebrate the kingdom's diversity and unity.

## Military and Defense
A well-disciplined military of professional soldiers, knights, and specialized units trained in conventional warfare and magical defense. A network of forts and garrisons guards the borders, and patrols keep trade routes safe. Valoria's navy won the [[War of the Distant Shore]].

## Religion
Valoria promotes religious freedom and tolerance; temples and shrines to many deities dot the landscape.

## Challenges
Occasional border disputes, political intrigue from rival factions, and the growing influence of magical organizations like the [[Arcane Concordance]].

## Places
### Hollow Quarter {district}
Beneath the grandeur and innovation of Valoria lies the Hollow Quarter: an expansive, densely populated undercity built into the bones of abandoned infrastructure, early experiments in [[Arcane Engineering]], and cultural enclaves left behind by progress. It isn't a place of darkness and despair but of mismatched opportunity, untamed invention, and simmering frustration. Valoria's rapid advancement has created inequality, not through corruption but through exclusion.

**Core tensions**
1. **Educational disparity:** magical and technical education is restricted to elite upper-city institutions; travel, tuition, and prerequisites are out of reach. Self-taught mages, rogue scholars, and folk inventors thrive here but lack legitimacy and safety nets, and many are exploited or dismissed. Underground schools, shared libraries, and teaching circles have formed, vulnerable to raids, sabotage, and misinformation.
2. **Progress leaves people behind:** workers, traditional artisans, and older generations have been displaced by automation and enchantment-driven innovation, such as laborers who can't compete with rune-operated machines.
3. **Cultural erasure:** the Quarter shelters forgotten traditions, minor religions, ancestral magics, and dialects that don't fit the city's central vision. Customs like ritual magic or organic alchemy are labeled "unregulated" or "inefficient."
4. **Unregulated Arcane Engineering:** a testbed for dangerous or brilliant inventions built from repurposed enchantments, discarded prototypes, and scavenged parts. Accidents are frequent, and successful innovations are often stolen or rebranded by upper-tier authorities. *"We build the future in secret, and they call it theft when it works."*
