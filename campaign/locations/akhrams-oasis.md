## Location
Deep within the [[Great Desert]], where shifting sands obscure its exact coordinates, lies Akhram's Oasis: a mirage-like city in the eye of a perpetual sandstorm, a sanctuary for outcasts, criminals, and those fleeing the grasp of powerful authorities.

## Description
The Oasis appears as if conjured from the desert itself. Buildings of sandstone and adobe twist and curve with the winds that whip through its narrow streets. Colorful banners mark bustling marketplaces where merchants from distant lands trade exotic goods: spices, silks, magical artifacts, and whispered secrets.

## Political Neutral Ground
At the heart of the Oasis is a single rule: **"Don't fuck with Thena."** [[Thena]], feared and respected, rose to power through cunning and powerful allies. She governs with charisma and calculated ruthlessness, ensuring rival factions can negotiate and trade freely under her watchful gaze. Political rivals from across kingdoms gather here to settle disputes and broker deals, shielded from the long arm of justice.

## The Teeth
Thena's authority is bolstered by the Guardians, known on the streets as **[[Faythe's Guardians|The Teeth]]**: enigmatic magical sentinels who patrol the borders and alleys, loyal only to Thena and her vision of the Oasis as a bastion of independence and power. *"Watch out for the Teeth, you never know what they hear."*

{secret}The Guardians were crafted through a pact with [[Faythe Kazula]], the elusive founder of the [[Arcane Concordance]]. Thena is unaware of Faythe's grand designs.{/secret}

## Thena's Ambitions
Thena's ambitions are rooted in securing her own power and protecting the Oasis's precarious balance. She sees it as a haven for those who defy convention, where strength and cunning decide one's place, and through alliances forged in shadow she seeks to expand its influence across the desert and beyond.

## Life in the Oasis
Scholars delve into forbidden tomes in hidden libraries. Adventurers and mercenaries swap tales in smoky taverns. Smugglers and spies deal in secrets and contraband under the wary gaze of the Teeth.

## Magic and the Law
- **No political influence:** magic used to manipulate negotiations, sway leaders, or undermine Thena's rule is one of the few hard laws, punished by imprisonment or death.
- **Artifacts and forbidden knowledge:** a grey area. Merchants quietly deal in enchanted weapons, cursed relics, and tomes of lost magic, but the Teeth watch those who delve too deep.
- **Wild magic and the wastelands:** the sandstorms and shifting sands carry volatile magical energy, and outsiders come to tap the chaotic ley lines beneath the city. Thena allows wild magic, but not as a destabilizing force.
