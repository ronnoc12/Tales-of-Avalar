## Overview
A far-off volcanic desert so unstable that few venture there: jagged obsidian spires, sulfur vents, and silent ashstorms. Travelers risk **leyline rifts** that randomly twist gravity or time. Reaching it from [[Valoria]] takes about 2.5 weeks round trip, with survival checks and heat resistance. {secret}The [[Ashvault Sanctum]] lies here.{/secret}
