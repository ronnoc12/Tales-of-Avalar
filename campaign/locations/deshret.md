## Overview
A historic desert city-state in the [[Great Desert]], ruled by a matriarchy known as the Daughters of Deshret. It was one of the few powers to survive [[The Sundering]].

During the [[Era of Darkness]], mages in Deshret were systematically persecuted; the city founded the Udjat Camps to imprison and exploit them (see [[Founding of Deshret and the Udjat Camps]]).
