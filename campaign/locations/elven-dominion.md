## Overview
A great empire of the [[Classical Era]], renowned for its mastery of nature magic and ageless beauty. The elves built vast, stunning, towering cities of crystal, hidden within dense forests and other hard-to-reach places.

The Dominion fought the [[Dwarven Kingdoms]] in the [[War of the Aether Crystals]], and later collapsed (see [[Fall of the Elven Dominion]]). Its misuse of the [[Aether Crystals]] is believed to have caused [[The Sundering]].
