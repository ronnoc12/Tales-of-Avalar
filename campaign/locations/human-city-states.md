## Overview
Sprawling city-states built by humans in the [[Classical Era]]. Ever adaptable, they traded and warred with both the [[Elven Dominion]] and the [[Dwarven Kingdoms]] for control of resources.

## Today
[[Sanctum]] is a surviving human city from this age. Its clerics were even more prevalent before the Sundering.
