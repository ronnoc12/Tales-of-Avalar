## Location
Lei-aluran lies in the heart of a vast, snow-covered, nearly impassable mountain range, surrounded by towering jagged peaks: one of the most isolated places in Avalar. As travelers approach the capital, the harshness fades. The snow melts, the air warms, and flora and fauna come alive in a perpetual spring, a result of the elves' deep connection to the natural world.

## History and Magic
Founded long ago by elven druids seeking a sanctuary untouched by the chaos of the outside world. The magic that creates the eternal spring is part of the elves' ancient connection to nature: a passive protective aura that shields the city from harsh environmental forces. It grows stronger when many elves gather, making the area a haven for druids and other magic users.

The elves are long-lived and deeply spiritual. Not all are druids, but most practice some form of nature magic. Their society is built on harmony with the environment, reverence for ancient traditions, and deep knowledge of the natural and magical worlds.

## Government
[[Queen Amaphia]] rules with beauty, grace, and wisdom, and governs with fairness and compassion. She is a spiritual leader as well as a monarch, guiding her people through the cycles of nature and the mysteries of magic.

Her consort, [[Feyrith]], holds no formal title, preferring to serve as her confidant and companion. Their bond of mutual respect and love is rare and treasured among the long-lived elves.

Her childhood friend [[Alaris]] is her most trusted advisor: her closest confidant and a skilled diplomat. {secret}He has long hidden his love for her out of respect for her union with Feyrith; his emotional turmoil may play a role in future political intrigue.{/secret}

## Political Significance
Isolation has kept Lei-aluran safe from Avalar's turmoil, but also makes it a target for those seeking power. Its internal politics and foreign relations have always been peaceful, but tensions over magical control and outside threats may change that. Its magical prowess makes it both ally and rival to other powers, especially the [[Celestial Alliance]].

## The Assassination Plot {secret}
Someone within the kingdom is plotting to kill Queen Amaphia. She remains unaware of the danger.

**Hook:** Alaris hires the party to investigate the plot. They may uncover a deeper conspiracy tied to ancient artifacts or factions seeking to control Lei-aluran's magical forces. Alaris's eventual self-sacrifice to save Amaphia could play a pivotal role in the kingdom's emotional and political future.

## Magic in Lei-aluran
- **Trained mages** focus on restorative, elemental, and nature-based magic: enhancing the environment, healing, and preserving the balance of life, with emphasis on control and sustainability. They have wide leeway, but reckless magic risks exile or imprisonment.
- **Wild magic zones:** some ancient sacred groves and secluded valleys are restricted to the most trusted mages and druids. Untrained users are believed to draw uncontrolled energy from the land there, causing outbursts and volatile phenomena.
- Only trained, sanctioned magic users may perform magic in public or for official purposes.
