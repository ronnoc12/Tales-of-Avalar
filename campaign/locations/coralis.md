## Location
A bustling coastal city on a tranquil bay, the gateway between Avalar's mainland and distant regions across the sea. Its architecture of elegant towers, floating platforms, and intricate canals reflects its mastery of both magic and engineering. It has long had a close relationship with the University of Magic on the opposite shore, with regular trade and knowledge exchange across the water.

## Government
A council of mages and merchants governs, headed by the Archmage of the Tides, a powerful and learned wizard. The council keeps Coralis at the forefront of magical research and trade, balancing prosperity with the growth of magical knowledge.

## Magic
Practitioners of all levels are welcome: arcane scholars, traders in enchanted goods, and adventurers honing their craft. The trade route across the bay brings a constant flow of magical knowledge and goods, making Coralis a critical player in Avalar's magical landscape.

## Culture
- **Academic influence:** universities, magical guilds, and research institutions draw scholars and students from all over the realm.
- **Cultural exchange:** ideas, art, and magic flow freely. Festivals of light and arcane art often fill the skies.

## Economy
- **Magical trade hub:** enchanted artifacts, arcane potions, and other magical goods; the University drives much of its innovation.
- **Global trade:** its location allows regular trade with distant lands and export of rare magical resources.
