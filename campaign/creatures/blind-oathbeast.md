## Overview
An earlier design for the Ashvault Sanctum's guardian, summoned by the [[Unsighted Order]] through planar rites banned since the fall of the Star Colleges. A fallen celestial wyrmling, twisted into a monstrous amalgam by [[Vhallion]]'s magic and the Order's failed attempts to recreate dragons.

{prep}Is the Oathbeast cut in favor of the Reverse Medusa, or a second guardian?{/prep}

## Appearance
- A featureless faceplate of obsidian fused over a jaw of human and dragon teeth.
- Wings of shredded scrolls, eternally burning.
- A voice like a choir of melted brass.

## Abilities
- Sees through the heat signatures of **guilt** and **shame**.
- **Soul Echo:** mimics the voice of a loved one calling for help, drawing prey closer.
- **Ashstorm Pulse:** a roar that burns the air and blinds anyone with a soul.
