## Overview
Vast tree-like entities of the [[Age of Primordial Chaos]], and living embodiments of Avalar's nascent elements. Once an aquatic species thriving in Avalar's oceans, they were forced onto land by the changing atmosphere. Their arrival altered the balance of elemental forces, and their constant consumption of oxygen provoked the [[Charr]] into a devastating war.
