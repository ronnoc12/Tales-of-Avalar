## Overview
The Era of the Shattered Veil marks a pivotal turning point in Avalar's history. As [[The Shattering|the Pearl of Avalar shatters]] and the veil between planes thickens, the material world and the planes are no longer easily traversed. The once-fluid boundaries are now harder to cross, limiting the flow of primordial energies and preventing frequent interaction with powerful planar entities.

This era sees the rise of the first modern races, as hunter-gatherer tribes and nomadic groups slowly evolve into the beginnings of organized civilizations. These new peoples tap into the elemental magics left behind by the primordial forces, using them to craft tools, weapons, and rudimentary spells that aid their survival and growth.

## Key Events
1. **[[The Shattering]]**: the [[Pearl of Avalar]] shatters, thickening the veils between the planes.
2. **The Formation of the [[Obelisks]]**: shards of the Pearl embed deep into the landscape and become towering crystalline Obelisks.
3. **Emergence of Modern Races**: as primal chaos settles into more defined patterns, the first modern races emerge from the remnants of older civilizations, a mixture of leftover elemental energies and newer, more structured forms of life.
4. **Harnessing the Primordial Energies**: remnant primordial magics (earth, fire, air, water, and aether) concentrate in certain regions, especially near the Obelisks.
   - **Elemental magic use:** early humans, elves, and dwarves harness these energies for tools and basic magic, mainly utility and survival: fire for warmth, earth for shelter, water to cultivate crops.
   - **First civilizations:** scattered tribes coalesce into the first towns and cities near Obelisks or places rich in primordial energy, using the elements to establish trade routes, build fortifications, and defend against the powerful creatures that once roamed freely.
5. **The Rise of the First Kingdoms**: early kingdoms form around the Obelisks, where the veil is most accessible. The first true rulers can wield magic not just as a tool but as a weapon; these early kings and queens often descend from the first tribes and grow powerful through control of these magical sites.
