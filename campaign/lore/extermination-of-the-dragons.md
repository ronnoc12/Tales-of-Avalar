## Overview
As fear of all magic worked itself into a frenzy, humanoids everywhere began to expand and indulge their paranoia, fearing creatures with innate magical abilities. This led to the systematic and aggressive genocide of dragons.

Chromatic dragons and humans had always been at odds over resources and territory, but now armies from multiple neighboring city centers banded together to hunt and exterminate this perceived magical threat. They believed dragons were innately magical beings that not only possessed magic but conjured or collected it to fuel their abilities. Some even believed that exterminating the dragons would cure humanity's magical afflictions entirely.

They were wrong, of course. The dragons have long since fled or been slain, forced into unfamiliar planes and the farthest reaches beyond civilized society. They are now believed to be extinct. {secret}This is not actually true: many found ways of hiding or extending their lifespans far beyond their years.{/secret}

## The Fae and the Dragons
Many dragons fled to the [[Feywild]], where some found refuge and integrated with the Fae. Others were too proud, angry at being displaced by "vermin" such as humanoids, and took their frustrations out on the Fae, trying to force them to give up their territories to the dragons.

But the Fae are not to be trifled with. They fought back hard, and the chromatic dragons who could not be reasoned with were dealt with, at great sacrifice. Many Fae lost their lives and their homes, but a brave few banished or killed the invading force, preserving the independence of the Feywild, for now.
