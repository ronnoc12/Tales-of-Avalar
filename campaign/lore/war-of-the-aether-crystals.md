## Overview
A conflict spanning centuries, this war erupted over the discovery of rare [[Aether Crystals]], which held vast magical properties capable of manipulating the very fabric of reality. Elves and dwarves fought fiercely for control of these resources, and the conflict is often cited as one of the reasons for the eventual collapse of many of the ancient empires.
