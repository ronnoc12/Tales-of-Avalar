## Overview
When the [[Pearl of Avalar]] shattered ([[The Shattering]]), its shards scattered across Avalar and embedded deep into the landscape. These shards are immense magical artifacts, each able to alter the flow of magic around it. Over time they became the Obelisks: towering structures of crystalline energy pulsing with the remnants of primordial magic.

## Properties
- They mark places where the veil between the planes is most accessible, and places of significant magical power.
- They can amplify or suppress magical abilities within their range, creating hotspots of intense magical potential.
- Some can warp the laws of nature, turning fertile land into desert, or the reverse.
- They can tame wild magic: the [[Unsighted Order]]'s [[Discovery of the Obelisks|discovery of this]] began the [[Reformation Era]].
- The first kingdoms formed around them, and the [[Arcane Concordance]] places its campuses and research centers near them, using them both to suppress raw magic and to amplify those who learn to harness them.

They are revered, feared, and studied.
