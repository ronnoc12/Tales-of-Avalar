## Sides
- The Kingdom of [[Valoria]], known for its strategic location and naval prowess.
- The [[Dominion of Sarnath]], a powerful empire known for expansionist policies and formidable magical capabilities.

## Reasons for Conflict
The war erupted over control of a strategic island chain in the middle of the Great Sea, believed to house relics of an ancient civilization, including artifacts that could shift the balance of power in the realm. Valoria sought them to bolster its defenses and keep its status as a regional power; Sarnath sought them to extend its dominion and enhance its magical arsenal.

## Course of the War
The war began with skirmishes over the smaller islands, leading up to the main conflict over the central island, which housed the most potent artifacts. Valoria deployed a large naval fleet and ground forces; Sarnath used powerful magical constructs and summoned otherworldly creatures. Both sides suffered heavy casualties.

## Turning Point and Victory
Valoria's forces, under [[Admiral Lucian Stormbringer]], launched a daring amphibious assault on the main citadel of Sarnath's forces. Through tactical brilliance, coordinated strikes, and the resilience of Valoria's troops, they breached the citadel and secured the artifacts.

## Aftermath
- The victory solidified Valoria as a military and naval power, letting it enhance its magical defenses and technology with the recovered artifacts.
- The Dominion of Sarnath, weakened by the loss of its artifacts and much of its military, retreated to regroup within its borders.
- The war was a stark reminder of the dangers of unchecked expansionism and ancient magical artifacts, leading to an uneasy peace as kingdoms reevaluated alliances.
- The recovered artifacts are still studied and used, influencing magical and military technology across the realm.
