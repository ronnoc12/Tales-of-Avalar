## Overview
Centuries after the Reformation, the [[Ameliorate]] had not only reintegrated into society but had advanced technology through their mastery of magic.

## Key Events
- **Rise of the [[Arcane Concordance]]**: splintering from the [[Unsighted Order]] (which still exists in its own right), the Arcane Concordance became a prestigious university and research center led by [[Faythe Kazula]], a visionary who believed in elevating magic users to positions of influence and respect. It became a beacon of knowledge and innovation, pushing the boundaries of magical and scientific understanding.
- {secret}**Rise of the [[Obsidian Order]]**: in contrast to the benevolent goals of other organizations, the Obsidian Order delved into darker pursuits, seeking forbidden powers, including a genetically modified parasitic organism capable of controlling magical creatures. Flaws in their creation threaten to unravel their plans, leading to internal strife and potentially catastrophic consequences.{/secret}
- [[War of the Distant Shore]]

## Current Tensions
Despite the strides made by the Arcane Concordance, opposition to magic persists. A faction among the formerly persecuted mages, embittered by past injustices, has formed a radical group demanding restitution for years of suffering and discrimination. Their growing numbers and radical ideology threaten to destabilize the fragile peace that has held for generations.

- **Rumblings of unrest:** radical factions seek restitution through extreme measures. The world teeters on the brink of new conflicts as ancient enmities and power struggles threaten fragile alliances.
- **The search for balance:** organizations like the [[Celestial Alliance]] strive to maintain harmony, facing ethical dilemmas in navigating magical and political intrigue.

The world of mages and non-mages has evolved from fear and persecution to tentative coexistence and advancement, but deep-seated grievances and ideological differences threaten to unravel centuries of progress.
