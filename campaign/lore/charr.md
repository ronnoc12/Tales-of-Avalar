## Overview
Beings made of flame in the [[Age of Primordial Chaos]], and living embodiments of Avalar's nascent elements. Born of fire, they had no natural affinity with the [[Sylvani]]'s growing dominance over the air, and attacked them in a brutal war that scorched the earth and split continents.
