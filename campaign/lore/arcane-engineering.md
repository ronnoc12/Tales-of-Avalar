## Overview
The formal, universally recognized term for the fusion of magic and technology. [[Valoria]]'s lightning-fast advances in Arcane Engineering and technomancy drive its prosperity, and its inequality.

## Other Names
- **Manacraft / Glyphworks / Runeware:** regional or subcultural slang in the Hollow Quarter.
- **Aetherfusion:** an esoteric or philosophical term used in older magical traditions.
- "Magitech" is an old term, now replaced.
