## The Legend
Stories tell of great adventurers who learned that the lich [[Vhallion]] had taken King [[King Alaric III|Alaric]], and faced three choices to free him: sacrifice a soul to rebind the lich to his box, destroy his phylactery, or each give part of their own soul to bind him.

Foolishly, the stories say, they chose to destroy the phylactery. They set out to find it, and on the way home they vanished mysteriously, never able to finish their quest.

Much of what they knew has been lost to time, and survives only as stories and legends of its own.

## The Truth {secret}
These were the previous party. They reached the [[Ashvault Sanctum]] and learned far more than the legends remember.

The legend is only half right. They did fail to finish their quest and never came home, but they succeeded far enough that the fey could take over after their disappearance: [[Vhallion's Phylactery|the phylactery]] reached the fey, who destroyed it using the material it was crafted from, dragon's blood. Their disappearance prompted world-altering decisions.
