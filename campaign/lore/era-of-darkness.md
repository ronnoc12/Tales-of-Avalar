## Overview
Following [[The Sundering]], the world plunged into an age of darkness and chaos. Magical energies surged unpredictably, spawning wild monsters and strange phenomena. Mages were feared and persecuted, blamed for the cataclysm. Cities erected barriers and wards to protect themselves from magical incursions, isolating and marginalizing magic users.

A few resilient powers survived the Sundering into this era, such as the [[Kingdom of Keldor]] and the [[Daughters of Deshret]].
