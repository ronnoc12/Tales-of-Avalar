## Overview
The Sundering marked a decisive moment in Avalar's history and is year zero of the calendar (years are counted After the Sundering, AS).

A catastrophic event triggered by a magical experiment gone awry: in their search for power and eternal life, the elves sought to tap into forbidden magics using the [[Aether Crystals]] (see [[Fall of the Elven Dominion]]).

## Consequences
- Avalar's continents fractured. Parts of the world spiraled into the void, while others were raised into the sky.
- Great tectonic shifts tore through the lands, leaving uninhabitable wastelands and creating new seas and mountains.
- Entire civilizations were wiped out, and the magical ley lines that once provided power became chaotic and unpredictable.
- The world entered a dark age as all the major civilizations collapsed or withdrew into isolation, beginning the [[Era of Darkness]].
- A few resilient powers survived, such as the [[Kingdom of Keldor]] and the [[Daughters of Deshret]].
