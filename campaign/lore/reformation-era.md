## Overview
Amid centuries of persecution, mages found a path to control their chaotic energies, beginning a safer era for magic users.

## Key Events
- **Rise of the [[Unsighted Order]]**: a nomadic group of marked mages, led by a visionary whose name is now lost to history, dedicated themselves to finding a way for mages to harness and control their chaotic energies for positive purposes. They found it in [[Discovery of the Obelisks|the Obelisks]], which could tame wild magic, laying the foundation for a safer world for magic users and beginning the Reformation.
- **Establishment of the [[Celestial Alliance]]**: witnessing rising magical instability and extra-planar threats, the Celestial Alliance was founded under the guidance of a powerful celestial deity. Its mission was to investigate the root causes of these disturbances and provide humanitarian aid. The Alliance quickly gained political influence and became a beacon of hope in times of crisis.
- **The Order of Nullification becomes [[The Enlightened]]**: originally known for zealous persecution of mages, the Order rebranded as The Enlightened during the Reformation and holds its stronghold in [[Sanctum]].
- **Founding of the [[Wardens of Ethereal Balance]]**: former members of the Unsighted Order who believed in a balanced approach to magic founded the Wardens to keep harmony between magic and mundane existence.
