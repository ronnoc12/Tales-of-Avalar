## Overview
Rare crystals holding vast magical properties, capable of manipulating the very fabric of reality. Their discovery sparked the centuries-long [[War of the Aether Crystals]] between elves and dwarves, and their misuse by the elves is believed to have caused [[The Sundering]].

## In the Feywild
Only a few aether crystals remain in the [[Feywild]], held by its archfey. They stabilize a traveler's flow of time, without which a few miles of travel could cost weeks or years on the Material Plane. Each archfey trades or tests for theirs according to their nature (see [[Feywild Archfey Council]]).
