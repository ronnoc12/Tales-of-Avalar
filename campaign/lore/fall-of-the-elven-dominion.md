## Overview
The collapse of the [[Elven Dominion]] was slow and tragic, worsened by internal strife, environmental change, and overuse of magic. The once-mighty empire fractured as factions vied for control of the fading magical essence of their lands.

Some scholars believe [[The Sundering]] was triggered by the elves' misuse of the [[Aether Crystals]]: the elves tried to violate the nature of mortality using the crystals, and were struck down for their hubris.
