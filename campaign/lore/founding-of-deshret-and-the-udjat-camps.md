## Overview
In the desert city of [[Deshret]], mages were systematically persecuted. The Udjat Camps were established to imprison and exploit mages, stripping them of their tongues and mutilating their hands to prevent spellcasting. This brutal practice continued for centuries, leaving a legacy of fear and resentment among magic users.
