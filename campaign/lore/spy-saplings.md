## Overview
Spy saplings are magically grown fey trees that observe, eavesdrop, and gossip for their archfey: technically alive, legally innocent, emotionally terrible liars. [[Lady Morivelle]]'s crystal favor involves them.

## How to Spot One
1. **Compliment test:** a spy sapling blushes, leans closer, and whispers *"Oh, well, no one's ever noticed before..."* If it says anything unprompted, it's a spy.
2. **Secret test:** announce a fake secret ("The moon owes me money"). Within 1d4 minutes a squirrel repeats it, a mushroom giggles, or the wind whispers it back wrong. Follow the gossip trail.
3. **Questions plants shouldn't answer:** "What did you hear earlier?" A spy pauses too long, leaves pointing slightly toward what it's watching: *"I couldn't possibly say... but if someone WERE watching, they'd be over there."*
4. **Rhythm:** spy saplings sync to fey courts; if a tree keeps time better than the bard, it's compromised.
5. **Hat test:** put a hat on it. A normal sapling drops it; a spy sapling adjusts it. If it tips the hat, that's espionage.

## When Confronted
They don't want to die, don't understand why spying is wrong, and bargain ("I only report the boring stuff?" "What if I lie?"). They fake fainting, pretend badly to be ordinary trees, and blame the mushrooms.

## Non-Lethal Solutions
- **Reassignment:** replant upside-down, move them somewhere loud, or put them near dramatic people.
- **Disguise:** paint eyes on all saplings, or give them all hats (they unionize).
- **Corruption:** feed them wrong information, or make them obsessed with something useless (hats, ducks, a fake prophecy).

The Fey tolerate the problem because spy saplings are adorable, gather excellent gossip, and no one wants to admit they lost control of their trees.
