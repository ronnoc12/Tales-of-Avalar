## Overview
In the distant past, the world was a realm of untamed magic and primal forces. Mythical creatures roamed freely, and the veil between planes was thin, allowing frequent interplanar crossings. Great primordial beings walked the planes, freely crossing between their boundaries. This era is shrouded in mystery and legend, with tales of powerful beings and cataclysmic events shaping early civilizations.

Avalar was a world in flux, constantly shaped by the raw, untamed forces of magic and nature.

## The Sylvani and the Charr
The [[Sylvani]], vast tree-like entities, and the [[Charr]], beings made of flame, were not simply peaceful races: they were the living embodiments of Avalar's nascent elements.

The Sylvani had once been an aquatic species, thriving in Avalar's vast oceans, but the planet's changing atmosphere forced them onto land. Their arrival created an unprecedented ecological shift, altering the balance of elemental forces.

The Charr, born of fire, had no natural affinity with the Sylvani's growing dominance over the air. The Sylvani's constant consumption of oxygen weakened the once-harmonious relationship between the elements, and the Charr attacked the Sylvani in a brutal, devastating war that scorched the earth and split continents.

## The Pearl of Avalar
It is said the [[Pearl of Avalar]], a magical artifact created during this war, was the key to both the Sylvani's and the Charr's eventual fall. The Pearl shattered ([[The Shattering]]), scattering its essence throughout Avalar and leaving behind dangerous relics, strange phenomena, and magical scars that would affect the world for eons.
