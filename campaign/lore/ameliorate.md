## Overview
The Ameliorate are mages certified by the [[Arcane Concordance]] after completing its training and final trial. They are considered trusted, productive members of society and are encouraged to reintegrate into mainstream culture, using their magic for the betterment of their communities.

## The Mark of the Ameliorate
A distinctive mark set in the center of the mage's dominant hand: a diamond-shaped shard of black [[Amisite]] in a golden setting, with cracks of red magical energy pulsing through it. It marks the mage as proof of their education and achievement.

## The Trial (what the party knows)
The exact methods of the trial are a closely guarded secret, not discussed with outsiders and rarely with peers. Each student undergoes a week-long trial in which they consume an elixir that enhances their connection to [[The Weave|the weave]], making their magic exceptionally potent yet unstable. They are tested emotionally, physically, and spiritually under extreme conditions to ensure their mastery and control of their abilities is absolute.

Before the trial, the student agrees to a magical binding afterward, which protects and shelters their mind from what they endured in the trial and permanently incorporates the Mark into their body.

## The Truth of the Binding {secret}
The binding is memory-modification magic that safeguards the university's secrets, not protection for the graduate. The public and the party believe it shelters the mind. Only a very rare few know the truth: those who miraculously resist the spell (a natural 20).
