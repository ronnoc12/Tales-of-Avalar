## Overview
As the world recovered from the primordial chaos, mortal civilizations flourished and great empires arose from the remnants of earlier wars. Each race developed unique magical traditions, architectural marvels, and expansive realms. The era was marked by cultural exchange, trade, and occasional conflicts over resources and territory.

## The Great Empires
- **The [[Elven Dominion]]**: renowned for mastery of nature magic and ageless beauty, the elves built vast, stunning, towering cities of crystal, hidden within dense forests and other hard-to-reach places.
- **The [[Dwarven Kingdoms]]**: dug deep into the mountains; known for craftsmanship, mining, and forging powerful enchanted metals.
- **The [[Human City-States]]**: ever adaptable, humans built sprawling city-states that traded and warred with both elves and dwarves for control of resources.
