## Overview
The Shattering is a cataclysmic moment in Avalar's history, closing the [[Age of Primordial Chaos]] and opening the [[Era of the Shattered Veil]].

The [[Pearl of Avalar]], a vast and mysterious artifact of immense magical power, was once the focal point of magical and planar energy. It served as a conduit that kept the veil between the planes thin, allowing the frequent movement of energies and beings across the realms.

During the final days of the Primordial Era, the Pearl shattered under the weight of a cosmic struggle. The reasons remain a mystery, but the consequences were immediate and profound:
- The veils between the planes thickened dramatically, making interplanar travel more difficult and dangerous.
- Magical creatures, demigods, and planar entities that once walked freely among mortals were trapped within their realms, unable to interact as directly as before.
- The rupture rippled through the very fabric of Avalar's magic.
- Shards of the Pearl scattered across Avalar and became the [[Obelisks]].
