## Overview
Good-aligned dragon god of justice and protection. Standard D&D deity: see the [Forgotten Realms wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bahamut).

## In This Campaign
The Ten Pillars of the Ember Hall show Bahamut creating dragonkind and later weeping in silence as his children are enslaved and exterminated; his temples fell as dragons were driven toward extinction ([[Extermination of the Dragons]]){secret}, a genocide [[Vhallion]] led{/secret}.
