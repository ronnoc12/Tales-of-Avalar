## Overview
Vhallion is a lich, now bound within [[King Alaric III]], escaped from the box that had held his soul (see Alaric's entry). A cult serves him; its symbols have been seen on his followers. {secret}He is Vhallion the Eternal Ember, known in Unsighted Order documents as "The Whispered Sovereign." Over a millennium ago he was High Thaumarch of the Sunken Empire of Meridas.{/secret}

## Personality {secret}
- **Arrogant intellectual:** convinced all mortal kingdoms are temporary, that freedom is chaos in disguise, and that centralized magical rule is not only superior but merciful. He doesn't see himself as evil; he sees himself as efficient. *"Empires fall. Heroes die. But I? I persist. Why else call me 'eternal' if not for the gift of foresight?"*
- **Darkly witty:** mocks heroism, sentimentality, and democratic ideals with deadpan delivery. *"Oh, you've come to stop me? I'm trembling. No, really, I might be. This body isn't holding up too well."*
- **Inevitability manifested:** resistance is futile, not because he is omnipotent but because he has outlived every kind of hero. *"You're the 'bold band of rebels,' right? Let me guess. The rogue is conflicted. The cleric's hiding something. The wizard thinks they're smarter than me. Adorable."*
- **Utilitarian tyrant:** he doesn't want to burn the world, he wants to run it better: dissent silenced, magic centralized, eternal order. *"You mistake my rule for cruelty. But show me one kingdom, just one, that has endured as long as I have. Peace through entropy is still peace."*

## Philosophy and Final Goal {secret}
He doesn't want scorched earth. He wants Valoria as the first city of his New Empire, ruled not by squabbling politicians or idealistic fools but by an immortal mind that has seen countless nations rise and fall. He genuinely believes:
- Magic should be controlled by the capable.
- Mortals will always ruin themselves unless guided by a single, unchanging will.
- Heroes are narrative illusions, not practical solutions.

## How He Shows Through Alaric
- Cutting, offhand remarks wildly out of character for the king.
- Laughing at suffering, but only when it is poetic or ironic, like a playwright admiring a tragic ending.
- Paranoia tinged with superiority: no one is smart enough to catch what is happening.
- Dreams of fire, endless throne rooms, and voices calling him "my lord."

## His Plan: Control Through Policy and Culture
He cannot seize the city at once, so he works from inside the king's subconscious to bend laws, magical theory, and public structure.
1. **Arcane Registry Mandate:** everyone with even a trace of magic must register and submit to "stability training," giving his cult lists of people to round up{secret}: the [[Unsighted Order]] turns them into mutated monsters{/secret}. *Hook:* scholars and Emberlight students disappear after testing.
2. **Soul Safety Ordinance:** bans unlicensed soulbinding, projection, and memory alteration, for civilians only, reserving that magic for himself and disarming anyone who could fight him on equal terms. *Hook:* an ally of the party is arrested for a minor memory ward on a patient.
3. **Unity Festival:** a crown-funded celebration of Valoria's "arcane heritage" that is really a ritual; hidden leyline amplifiers siphon its magic to strengthen his hold on the king. *Hook:* artificers building the displays feel drained or see visions of burning thrones.
4. **Purging the council:** pressuring the king to suspend councilors on fabricated treason charges. [[High Seeress Nyla Thorne]] is exiled. He cannot touch [[Lady Seraphine Dorne]]: Alaric resists any attempt to harm her, though he may treat her worse than he otherwise would.

## Weaknesses
1. Sacrifice another soul to guard him, rebinding him to the box.
2. Find and destroy his phylactery, lost to time. {secret}It was guarded by the Unsighted Order in the [[Ashvault Sanctum]]. The previous party recovered it and delivered it to the fey, who destroyed it with dragon's blood, the material it was crafted from.{/secret}
3. Each party member sacrifices part of their soul to bind him.

In legend, the great adventurers of [[The Unfinished Road]] chose to destroy the phylactery, and never returned.

## Crusade Against Dragonkind
Rumors say Vhallion led the extermination of the dragons. No one can say for certain.

{secret}It is true, and the fey know it. He slew [[Vortheryx]], the Eclipsed Flame, in the [[Ashvault Sanctum]], and the last of the Ember Hall's ten pillars, "The Mocking Sovereign," shows him triumphant over a dragon's remains: "Even gods bleed. Even dragons kneel." The only known record is in the Ashvault Sanctum, found by the previous party and perhaps lost again for a long time.{/secret}
