## Appearance
Elegant, with cracked-glass horns and bioluminescent tattoos that shift to mimic constellations. Her real name is unknown.

## Backstory
Raised in the city's orphanage system, she became a thief, then a spy. She is embedded in the [[Emberlight Collective]] as a patron of community art and education, and is a key manipulator in [[Sorcerer-Lord Velmion Drex|Velmion]]'s plans. She handed the box to [[King Alaric III]], disguised as a gift from a "foreign diplomat."

## Motivations
She truly believes Valoria is broken beyond repair and wants chaos to reveal people's true selves. To her, the Order is the only group honest enough to reshape the world.

## Weakness
She has developed an emotional bond with some Emberlight children and might, in a moment of weakness, hesitate to betray them fully.
