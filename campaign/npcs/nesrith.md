## Overview
Figurehead leader of the kobolds of [[Klag]]. She governs hands-off through a council of kobolds, acting as mediator and tie-breaker, and believes in letting the kobolds govern themselves. Under her leadership Klag supports [[Tornus]]'s rebellion in [[BrokRogGnakShragBromKur]].

## True Nature {secret}
She appears to be a blue dragonborn but is an adult blue dragon in disguise, deeply loyal to her people and rejecting the BBEG's manipulation of her family.
