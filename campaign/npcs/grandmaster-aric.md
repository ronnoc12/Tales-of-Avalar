## Background
From a lineage of diplomats and mediators, Aric showed a talent for negotiation and understanding diverse perspectives from a young age, and honed it under esteemed mentors in the Order. His calm demeanor and ability to find common ground define his leadership: harmony within the Order and peaceful resolution of even the hardest disputes.
