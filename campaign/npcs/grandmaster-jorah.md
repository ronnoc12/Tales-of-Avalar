## Background
A plague swept through Jorah's homeland, claiming his family and many others. Determined to prevent such suffering, he devoted himself to the healing arts, learning from renowned healers and herbalists across the realms. His leadership is defined by boundless empathy and a relentless drive to relieve suffering wherever he finds it.
