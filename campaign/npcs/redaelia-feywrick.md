## Appearance
Young, with a thin but muscled build. Wears thick glasses.

## Personality
Shy yet assertive, intelligent, efficient, and fiercely protective. She refuses to tolerate disrespect or mistreatment from anyone, whatever their stature, which sometimes leads her to speak out of turn.

## Background
A certified [[Ameliorate]], trained at the [[Arcane Concordance]], where she learned to control her innate abilities. She manages Casfer's schedule, communications, and operation coordination, and also joins field operations.

## Arcane Abilities
Focused on manipulating and controlling elemental forces, especially earth and fire. She is skilled in defensive spells and can summon protective barriers and elemental constructs. Combined with her physical strength and resilience, she is formidable in both office and field.
