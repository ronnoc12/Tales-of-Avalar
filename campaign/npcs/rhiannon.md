## Overview
**True name:** unknown. "Rhiannon" is a moniker; fey guard their true names closely.

**Domain:** fate, choices, crossroads, and the invisible threads that connect events. Her lands are full of shifting roads, labyrinthine forests, and bridges that appear or vanish depending on the traveler's decisions. She sees the world as a puzzle and herself as its subtle guide.

**Personality:** calculating, patient, meticulous. She rewards cleverness, insight, and ingenuity and enjoys watching mortals and fey navigate her puzzles, but never acts violently without reason.

## Crystal Favor
She lends an [[Aether Crystals|aether crystal]] only if the party completes a puzzle of choices in her domain: a shifting labyrinth, deciding whom to trust among illusions, or a moral dilemma with no objectively right answer.
- **Success** shows they can wield time-stabilizing magic responsibly.
- **Failure** has consequences: being trapped in a looping part of her domain, or being subtly altered (e.g. a party member starts seeing alternate outcomes of every decision).
- Her crystal stabilizes time just long enough to cross the Feywild safely. It must be returned, or she may call them back to finish the challenge properly.
