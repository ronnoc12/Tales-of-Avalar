## Background
Discovered as a child showing an innate connection to elemental energies, with spontaneous displays of natural magic. Taken in by the Eternal Wardens, she was trained by wise elemental masters to channel and control those energies responsibly. Her leadership is defined by reverence for the natural world, protecting its sanctity, and using elemental power for good.
