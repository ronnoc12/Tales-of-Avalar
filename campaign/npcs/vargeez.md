## Overview
When [[Triq]] left to seek power, Vargeez did his best to keep the clan alive, but many died. Desperate and starving, the tribe tried to hold up members of the [[Wanderers of the Wandering Grove|Wandering Grove]], hardly a threat after months of travel and hunger. Seeing the desperation in their eyes, the head of the Grove took pity on them, shared food and resources, and taught them to hunt and trap the animals of the land.

## Change of Heart
At first Vargeez meant to use the humans and betray them once his people had learned all they could. Every day he told himself tomorrow they would strike, and every morning the equality and kindness the Grove had shown them stopped him. How could he defile what his people had struggled so hard for?

Each conversation with the head of the Grove convinced him that acceptance had found them, and that asking his people to die to be accepted elsewhere was a fool's errand. Without Triq to fan the flames, most of the clan came around. Vargeez and many of the goblins now travel with the Grove, living in harmony with the land.

## Unrest {secret}
Some goblins still harbor hatred for the "pink skins," awaiting Triq's return or a new leader to take them to victory. If unchecked, this will eventually lead to a mutiny.
