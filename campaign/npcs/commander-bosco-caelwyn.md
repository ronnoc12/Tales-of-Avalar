## Appearance
Muscular and composed, with braided dark hair, bronze skin, and numerous tattoos. Wears surprisingly little armor: only leather studded with runes.

## Role
Leads Valoria's military, including elite arcane-engineering units, and plans border defense and peacekeeping.

## Politics and Magic
Moderate-conservative. Supports magic in warfare but insists on disciplined use. Opposes rogue magic factions and favors training soldiers in both spell and steel.
