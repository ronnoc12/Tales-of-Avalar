## Overview
A terrified goblin courier, once employed by [[Velkyn Solutions]] and captured near [[Midcross]]. A few weeks before the goblin siege he delivered a gift directly to the Valorian palace: a "funeral-jar-looking thing" that smelled of blood and roses and glowed cold, meant for King Alaric III and handed to [[Lady Alene Vorn]].
