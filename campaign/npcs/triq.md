## Overview
After the goblins' defeat at [[Midcross]] and the discovery of their home, Triq led her people south toward the lands of the [[Wanderers of the Wandering Grove|Wandering Grove]]. About 20% of them died on the way; some stayed behind, unwilling to travel. The wild, forested land was unlike their mountain home, with no easy targets, and more perished trying to rebuild.

Triq got her people to their new shelter, then decided she needed power to end their suffering, and left them to find it.

## The Pact with Orcus {secret}
She seeks out a long-forgotten temple to [[Orcus]], Demon Lord of Undeath, to find his grimoire and make a deal to save her people. Orcus gives her the power to resurrect her dead, empowering them to fight for those who remain. All of her constructs use hellfire steel to shuttle the souls of those killed to her patron.

It takes her a long time to find the temple and gain this power, but once she does she will return to raise her people from the dead and lead them once more.

- **Why Orcus:** raw destructive power and the power to raise her fallen. She refused to let the dead of the road go.
- **The cost:** Orcus demands souls; Triq would become his chief harbinger, culling life to feed his domain. Orcus loves despair, and her tragic fall is a feather in his cap.
- **The moral challenge:** "liberated" goblins who are actually undead.
