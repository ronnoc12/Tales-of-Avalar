## Appearance
Barefoot and soot-faced, with round goggles that glow when she lies. Her clothes jingle with charms and patched-together relics.

## Backstory
Grew up in a family that worshipped invention like a religion. Valoria's guilds seized her early machines, so she turned to the Hollow Quarter, where she has built a maze of light-tunnels and rail carts beneath the city.

## Motivations
She resents the upper city's claim that only sanctioned artifice is safe or meaningful.

## Secret {secret}
Her blueprints for a dimensional capacitor were stolen. She doesn't know the [[Unsighted Order]] used part of her design in the box delivered to the king.
