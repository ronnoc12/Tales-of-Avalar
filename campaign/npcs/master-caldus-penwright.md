## Appearance
Plump and nasal-voiced, with ink-stained fingers, pince-nez goggles, and a pocket watch that doubles as a minor chronomantic tool.

## Role
Manages trade policy, infrastructure development, and technological regulation.

## Politics and Magic
Mercantile-liberal. Sees magic as an economic engine. Champions the rights of artificers and inventors and advocates public access to arcane engineering, like transit and sanitation systems.
