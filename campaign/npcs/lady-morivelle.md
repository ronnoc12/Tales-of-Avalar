## Overview
**True name:** unknown. "Morivelle" is a moniker; fey guard their true names closely.

**Domain:** twisting, semi-sentient forests and overgrown brambles.

**Personality:** proud, calculating, vindictive, and deeply emotional; she sees insults as eternal wounds and distrusts anyone who trespasses in her forests.

## Crystal Favor
She will lend an [[Aether Crystals|aether crystal]] only if the party eliminates a rival's "intrusive [[Spy Saplings|spy saplings]]": magically grown trees that act as a domain's eyes and ears, disguised as ordinary plants. The task needs clever thinking: destroy them carefully without alerting other fey, or risk retaliation.
