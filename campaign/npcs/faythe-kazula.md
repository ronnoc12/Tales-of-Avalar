## Overview
The visionary founder and Archmage of the [[Arcane Concordance]], who elevated magic users to positions of influence and respect.

{prep}Older docs called her "Cassandra" (renamed). Decide her appearance and voice.{/prep}

## Origins
She was born into a society where magic users were feared and persecuted. Her noble-born parents were talented practitioners who hid their abilities to avoid persecution. When her own talent began to manifest young, her family drew growing suspicion and hostility from their neighbors.

One tragic night, a mob driven by fear and ignorance attacked her home, accusing her family of witchcraft. Her childhood home, her parents, and her older brother were burned alive; she barely escaped and fled into the wilderness. Alone and traumatized, she vowed to master her magic to protect herself.

Through years of study and hardship with secret magical texts and artifacts hidden by ancient civilizations, she learned to harness the raw, untamed power of magic, and met other magic users who had suffered similar fates.

## The Darkening {secret}
As her power grew, so did her ambition, and her vision darkened into a desire for retribution against those who had persecuted her and others like her. She founded the Concordance as a sanctuary and university, but secretly as a tool to infiltrate and manipulate the power structures of society. She believes non-magic users must face consequences for past injustices, and she will go to extreme measures to reshape the world in her image.

She also struck the pact that created the Guardians of [[Akhram's Oasis]] ([[Faythe's Guardians]]); [[Thena]] knows nothing of her designs.

Her story is a tragedy of revenge: a reminder of the dangers of prejudice, and of unchecked power.
