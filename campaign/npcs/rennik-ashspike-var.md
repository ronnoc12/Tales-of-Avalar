## Appearance
Broad-shouldered and gray-skinned, always in salvaged arc-steel armor etched with prayers to dead comrades. Missing one eye, replaced by a glowing, unstable crystal shard.

## Backstory
Once a Valorian soldier, discharged for questioning the use of experimental battlefield enchantments. Now he protects the Quarter with streetwise toughness and tactical brilliance.

## Motivations
He trains young Hollow Quarter residents in street defense and battlefield tactics, and is suspicious of all outsiders, especially those with power.

## Secret {secret}
The crystal in his eye is an early prototype [[Obsidian Order]] surveillance device, and it is slowly feeding his dreams to them.
