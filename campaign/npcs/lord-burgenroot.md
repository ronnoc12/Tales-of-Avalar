## Appearance
A tall, stout man with graying hair and a meticulously groomed beard framing his square jaw. Dresses practically: dark robes with discreet symbols of wealth and authority.

## Leadership
A staunch pragmatist who values tangible outcomes and economic stability above all, viewing [[Midcross]] primarily as a conduit for goods and cargo. He supports initiatives that bolster trade infrastructure and security, guided by economic forecasts and profit.

- **Skepticism and prudence:** little faith in abstract ideas; he wants practical solutions backed by evidence.
- **Authority:** ultimate authority in Midcross, upheld by an advisory council ([[Midcross Council]]). A unanimous council vote can remove him, despite growing dissent among some factions.

## Criticism and Public Perception
Respected for keeping Midcross secure and prosperous, but seen by some as aloof to the plight of the less fortunate. Programs for the poor are underfunded and badly managed, fueling a growing underclass, unrest, and activists pushing for social reform.

## Current Business
He has posted a bounty to learn why neither the lumber nor his last several representatives sent to [[Ravenwood]] have returned.
