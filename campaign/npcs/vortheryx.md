## Overview
A red wyrm philosopher-king who tried to rally the remaining dragons against their destroyer{secret}, [[Vhallion]]{/secret}. He was hunted, betrayed by a corrupted drakeblood envoy, and slain in his sacred den, the [[Ashvault Sanctum]]. His corpse was left brutalized; his skull, fused with a cursed iron spike, sits at the sanctum's center above his final words: *"They came not for our fire, but for our future."*
