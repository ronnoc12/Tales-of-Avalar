## Background
Kaelyn grew up in a small village ravaged by bandit attacks. Witnessing her people's suffering, she trained relentlessly in martial arts to defend her community and became a formidable warrior and protector. She joined the Eternal Wardens to extend that protection beyond her village. Her leadership is marked by unwavering dedication to justice and a fierce determination to shield the innocent.
