Once a renowned expert in Applied Glyph Theory at Valoria's Academy of Arcane Engineering. After a tragic lab accident {secret}(possibly sabotaged){/secret} she was cast out for "unstable magical judgment." Now she is revered in the Hollow Quarter as a brilliant, selfless teacher.

## Motivations
She hates the nobility's "ownership of knowledge."
