## Appearance
Bright red short hair, gelled and pulled back in a handsome, professional style. Looks younger than he is.

## Personality
Good-natured, wise, sharp-witted, and loyal. Despite his youthful appearance he has insight beyond his years, which carried him swiftly through USE's ranks. He blends persuasive charm with intimidating resolve when needed, and his bad jokes keep his team's camaraderie strong.

## Leadership
Paternal and protective toward his operatives, valuing their safety above all. He keeps a strong network of contacts and informants throughout [[Midcross]] to navigate its political and criminal landscape.

## Philosophy
Prefers subtlety and finesse, avoiding unnecessary conflict. When the stakes are high and diplomacy fails, he won't hesitate to take decisive action, including violence. He must balance loyalty to USE against the guild's need to stay neutral in Midcross politics.
