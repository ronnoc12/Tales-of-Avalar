## Appearance
Elderly but sharp-eyed, with a brass monocle enchanted for spell detection and a mechanical arm with spell-weaving capabilities.

## Role
Heads magical research, manages partnerships with magical institutions like the [[Arcane Concordance]], and funds innovation.

## Politics and Magic
Progressive-technocrat. A devout believer in magic as society's future. Pushes to fuse spellwork with steam engineering and opposes restrictions on magical experimentation.
