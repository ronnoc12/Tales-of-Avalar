## Background
Born with the gift of prophecy, Thaedus experienced vivid visions and dreams foretelling significant events. The Eternal Wardens' elder seers mentored him in harnessing and interpreting them. His deep connection to the celestial realm gives the Order invaluable foresight in times of uncertainty.
