## Overview
**True name:** unknown. "Nyxylia" is a moniker; fey guard their true names closely. {secret}[[Haldir]] knew her true name, in Sylvan; he learned it when they took their wedding vows.{/secret}

**Domain:** security, law, guardian of the [[Feywild#Crimson Vault|Crimson Vault]], dragon blood, and dragon population control.

**Personality:** stoic, pragmatic, terrifyingly competent, deeply intelligent and wise. She is respected and feared by the other archfey and rarely favors mortals, except for tasks that fit her strict stewardship.

Her husband [[Haldir]], her most trusted warlock, is dead.

## Crystal Favor
She owns only one [[Aether Crystals|aether crystal]] and cannot spare it without compromising her guardianship. If the party performs a task that stabilizes part of the dragon population or prevents a dangerous breach, she may lend it briefly or help in other ways. She is generous, but her domain always comes first.
