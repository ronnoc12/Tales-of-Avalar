## Overview
**True name:** unknown. "Toremona" is a moniker; fey guard their true names closely.

**Domain:** dreams, memory, shadows, and emotional landscapes.

**Personality:** mysterious, poetic, deeply emotional; obsessed with collecting the experiences of mortals and fey. Quiet, enigmatic, and endlessly curious about the psyche; sometimes aloof or playful with other archfey.

## Crystal Favor
She lends an [[Aether Crystals|aether crystal]] only in exchange for a personal memory or dream from a party member: a moment of joy, sorrow, or a deeply held hope. The exchange is permanent. She preserves it in her collection and may later use fragments of it to communicate with or manipulate the donor. Deciding what memory to offer can create rich tension in the party.
