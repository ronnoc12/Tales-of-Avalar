## Appearance
Ever-changing robes that shift with his mood: coppers, russets, and dying gold.

## Backstory
A disgraced philosopher-mage, once banished from the [[Arcane Concordance]] for "dangerous soulweaving theory." He now leads the Order's work in [[Valoria]] and orchestrated the delivery of the box to [[King Alaric III]].

## Motivations
To him, the lich's return is not domination but "controlled unity."

## Secret
He keeps a hidden vault of failed soul-fusion experiments, including one that still whispers to him at night, possibly a failed earlier attempt at housing the lich's spirit.
