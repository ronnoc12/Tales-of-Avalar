## Appearance
Ethereal and serene, with luminous eyes and robes that shimmer with soft, radiant glyphs. Always barefoot, claiming it helps her feel the ley lines.

## Role
Ensures religious tolerance, mediates disputes among faith groups, and monitors magical disturbances in the realm's spiritual balance.

## Politics and Magic
Apolitical / spiritual. Sees magic as a divine gift. Supports magic that aligns with moral and spiritual harmony, and warns against over-industrializing the arcane.

{secret}In Vhallion's takeover, she is the councilor exiled in Week 2.5 ("Exile of Councilor Thorne").{/secret}
