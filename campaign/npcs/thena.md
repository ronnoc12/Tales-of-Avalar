## Overview
The formidable ruler of [[Akhram's Oasis]], feared and respected. Her one rule: **"Don't fuck with Thena."** She governs with charisma and calculated ruthlessness, keeping the Oasis neutral ground where rivals can negotiate and trade.

## Early Life
Born into a nomadic tribe that traversed the unforgiving [[Great Desert]], skilled in trade and diplomacy. She learned survival young, along with negotiation and a keen intuition for reading people.

## Exile and Struggle
Her tribe was betrayed by a rival faction during a crucial trade negotiation and left stranded in the desert. Determined to survive and seek justice for her people, she crossed the desert alone, forging alliances with outcasts and renegades who shared her desire for freedom from outside control.

## Rise to Power
She stumbled on the elusive Oasis, hidden in the heart of a perpetual sandstorm, and saw its potential as a haven for the persecuted. Through shrewd negotiation and alliances with influential factions, she gradually became the figure who keeps order amid the chaos.

## Leadership
She rules with pragmatism and ambition, backed by loyal followers, skilled advisors, and the Guardians (The Teeth). {secret}She is unaware that the Guardians come from a pact with [[Faythe Kazula]], or of Faythe's grand designs.{/secret}
