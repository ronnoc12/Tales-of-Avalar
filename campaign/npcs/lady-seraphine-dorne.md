## Appearance
Tall and statuesque, with silver-streaked auburn hair worn in a braided crown. Wears tailored velvet coats lined with enchanted filigree.

## Role
Oversees domestic policy, legal systems, and administrative coordination across the kingdom.

## Politics and Magic
Centrist-pragmatic. A strong proponent of regulated magic: she believes integrating the arcane into civil institutions makes them fairer and more efficient, and advocates magical education for bureaucrats.

## Her Heart {secret}
She loves [[King Alaric III]], and he loves her. She fears confessing would damage their friendship, and believes he deserves someone of his standing, not a working-class childhood friend.
