## Appearance
The council's youngest member, with soot-streaked cheeks, red goggles pushed up on her forehead, and a steam-powered prosthetic leg. A former engineer and activist.

## Role
Voices the concerns of the working class and of urban and rural communities; promotes public projects and equitable policy.

## Politics and Magic
Populist-reformist. Advocates universal magical education, demystifying magic and using it for social mobility. Opposes elitist control of magical institutions.
