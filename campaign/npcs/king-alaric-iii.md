## Appearance
Once a figure of imposing stature and commanding presence, tall and regal in elaborate royal attire, with piercing eyes and a stern countenance. Lately he has grown gaunt, pale, and hollow-eyed; his hands tremble when idle, and he is surrounded by heavier-than-usual enchantments, warding runes, and whisper-guards.

## Background
From a long line of monarchs. He took the throne in his early twenties after the untimely death of his father, King Eldric, and quickly proved a capable, visionary leader. He rules alone, with no queen or heirs.

## Leadership
- **Wisdom and prudence:** weighs decisions carefully, seeks counsel, and considers long-term consequences.
- **Strength and diplomacy:** forges alliances and maintains Valoria's standing among the kingdoms.
- **Achievements:** economic growth through infrastructure, trade, agriculture, and industry; a modernized military; strong support for arts, literature, and education.
- **Values:** integrity, compassion, justice, and equality under the law.

## Seraphine
[[Lady Seraphine Dorne]] has been his best friend since childhood. {secret}They love each other truly and deeply, but neither has been bold enough to say so because of the gap in their pedigree. He has abandonment issues and fears that bringing her into his life would eventually drive her away.{/secret}

## The Lich Within
Alaric is possessed by the lich [[Vhallion]]. He was presented with a box, said to be a "symbol of eternal stability" and a gift from a foreign diplomat. The box had bound Vhallion's soul to the soul of a guardian entity; imprisoned together, Vhallion tortured the guardian until it willingly moved on, weakening the prison so that when Alaric opened the box, Vhallion escaped into him. The lich now feeds on his soul from within.

{secret}The box was delivered by [[Thorn of Glass]] on the orders of [[Sorcerer-Lord Velmion Drex]], agents of the [[Unsighted Order]], Vhallion's cult.{/secret}

- **Symptoms:** increasingly paranoid, plagued by intrusive thoughts, drained of strength. Cutting, out-of-character remarks; laughing at poetic or ironic suffering; dreams of fire, endless throne rooms, and voices calling him "my lord."
- **Ways to free him:**
  1. Sacrifice another soul to guard Vhallion, rebinding him to the box.
  2. Find and destroy his phylactery, lost to time. {secret}It was in the [[Ashvault Sanctum]]; the previous party recovered it and the fey destroyed it with dragon's blood.{/secret}
  3. Each party member sacrifices part of their soul to bind him.
- In legend, the great adventurers of [[The Unfinished Road]] chose the phylactery, and never returned.
- {secret}**Stakes:** if the lich gains full control, he holds the most powerful position in Valoria and uses the kingdom as the first city of his New Empire.{/secret}
- {secret}**Seraphine:** even as Vhallion's influence grows, Alaric resists any attempt to harm Lady Seraphine, though he may not treat her as well as he otherwise would.{/secret}
