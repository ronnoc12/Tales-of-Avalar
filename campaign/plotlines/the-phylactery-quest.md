## Overview
The quest behind the legend of [[The Unfinished Road]]: to free King Alaric, the adventurers chose to find and destroy [[Vhallion]]'s phylactery, a 2-3 week round trip into the middle of nowhere.

## What Really Happened {secret}
The previous party travelled to the [[Ashvault Sanctum]], faced its guardian, and recovered [[Vhallion's Phylactery]]. They vanished before finishing, but had done enough that the fey took over and destroyed it with dragon's blood.
