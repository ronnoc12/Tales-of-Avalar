## Overview
Over four to five weeks, [[Vhallion]] uses [[King Alaric III]]'s love for [[Lady Seraphine Dorne]], his fear of failure, idealism, and isolation to tighten his grip on the king and the kingdom.

## Vhallion's Emotional Bomb {secret}
Each week Vhallion tempts a different party member through dreams or magical contact, offering freedom from consequence, ultimate magical control, "protection" for someone they love, or a way to undo their worst failure. *"You all dream of change. I am the dream fulfilled. Why die resisting what you could become?"*
