## Overview
An investigative arc from the previous campaign: starting from a seemingly unrelated incident, the party peeled back layers of subterfuge to discover the king's possession. Per your ruling, [[Velkyn Solutions]] is a front for the [[Unsighted Order]] (the source text said Obsidian).

## The Midcross Connection
[[Councilor Drenth Thark]], a halfling trade minister with quiet gambling debts and a taste for magical curiosities, was offered "rare goblinoid antiquities" laced with compulsions. In what he believed was a private, enchanted discussion about trade routes, he leaked Midcross's border defense plans.

## Clue Trail
1. **Paper trail:** Velkyn's founding date precedes its first transaction by years, a long-built shell. Its arcane-transport permits are signed by a clerk who died six months earlier, and some bear glyph-seals of [[Lord Fenric Halden]]'s diplomatic office, without his signature: a compromised foreign office or high-level forgery.
2. **The Resonant Vault:** a Midcross-linked warehouse holds a shattered obsidian mirror ringed with runes (a failed communication device), caged minor magical creatures (some ill, some possessed), and a chalkboard on "soul transference latency." A coded research log: *"Progress with vessel nearing climax. Host stability confirmed. Regal bond established. Phase II awaits final bloom."*
3. **The courier:** [[Lirrek Slaadwhistle]], a goblin captured near Midcross, delivered a gift to the palace a few weeks before the goblin siege: a "funeral-jar-looking thing" that smelled of blood and roses and glowed cold, handed to [[Lady Alene Vorn]], a royal secretary who has since "gone on retreat."
4. **Emberlight:** [[Professor Telra Yenn]] recognizes parts of the design from stolen capacitor schematics co-opted through a fake research grant. *"Someone turned my prototype into a cage for a mind. If that was given to the king... you're not just dealing with political rot. You're dealing with possession."*
5. **The king's symptoms:** pale and forgetful; divination banned in the palace; surveillance of unregulated casters, especially in the Hollow Quarter; new advisors with no public record.

## Revelation
The king received the artifact from Velkyn; a lich spirit is bound to him; his instability is magical corruption, not political failing. The party may be the only ones who know, and speaking up could brand them traitors.

## Next Steps
- Smuggle a seer or spirit-talker into the palace to diagnose the king.
- Find Lady Alene Vorn, possibly captured or silenced.
- Infiltrate Velkyn's Valoria headquarters, guarded by controlled beasts and sigil-bound mercenaries.
- Track down a rival necromancer who might know how to sever the lich's bond, at a cost.
