## Overview
[[Tornus]], half-dwarf, half-duergar heir of two exiled leaders, leads a rebellion against the scheming dual kings, backed by [[Nesrith]]'s kobolds of [[Klag]]. Both cities are on the verge of open conflict, and the party could tip the scales either way. {secret}Along the way they can discover Tornus's true heritage.{/secret}
