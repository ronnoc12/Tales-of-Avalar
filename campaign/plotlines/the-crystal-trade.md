## Overview
Only a few [[Aether Crystals|aether crystals]] exist in the [[Feywild]], and without one time slips away by weeks or years. Each archfey of the [[Feywild Archfey Council]] trades or tests for theirs: [[Lady Morivelle]] (destroy a rival's spy saplings), [[Nyxylia]] (stabilize the dragon population), [[Rhiannon]] (a puzzle of choices), [[Toremona]] (a memory or dream, given forever).
