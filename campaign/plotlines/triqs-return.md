## Overview
After the goblins' defeat at [[Midcross]], [[Triq]] led her people south, then left them to seek power. [[Vargeez]] kept the survivors alive, and many now live peacefully with the [[Wanderers of the Wandering Grove|Wandering Grove]].

## The Pact {secret}
Triq seeks [[Orcus]]'s grimoire in a forgotten temple and gains the power to resurrect her dead; her hellfire-steel constructs shuttle the souls of the slain to Orcus. When she returns, she will raise her people and lead them again, a moral horror for any party: "liberated" goblins who are actually undead. Some goblins in the Grove still hate the "pink skins" and await her return; unchecked, this becomes a mutiny.
