## Overview
The [[Obsidian Order]] runs hidden labs beneath the Hollow Quarter, testing its unstable parasitic control organism on magical beasts and recruiting or abducting self-taught mages. The parasite's flaw, bursts of uncontrollable aggression, threatens to expose them.
